Dana lets people work together on shared pages and tasks.

## Invite someone

Open the relevant shared work area and use its Team or sharing controls. An invitation can grant an access level suited to the person's role.

Your private Desk cannot be shared.

## Access and responsibilities

**Access** controls which actions a teammate can perform in Dana. A written **Responsibility** describes what they are expected to do. Writing a responsibility does not grant application permissions.

Members of a shared work area can read its pages. Access to a single task is not granted independently of that shared area.

## Work with teammates

Use [teammate profiles](/help/teammate-profiles) for people and responsibilities. Use [Discussions and direct messages](/help/discussions-and-messages) for conversation.
