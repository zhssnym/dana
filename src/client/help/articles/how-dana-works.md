Dana keeps tasks and writing together, beside the work they belong to. Four ideas cover most of the app.

## Pages

A page is the home of one piece of work, such as a project, a topic, or a client. It holds writing, tasks, and nested pages. The **Library** in the sidebar lists your pages.

## Facets

A page shows its content in **Facets**, the tabs at the top of the page. A Work Facet is for tasks and progress. A Doc Facet is for notes and other writing. Use **Add Facet** to add one, and rename or reorder Facets in the strip. A deleted Facet goes to Trash, and you can restore it.

## Tasks

A task is work that someone can finish. It can have assignees, a Start Date, a Due Date, and its own notes. A checklist in your writing is only text. Use a task when the work must be assigned, scheduled, or completed. See [Tasks](/help/tasks-and-dates).

## Your Desk and shared work

Your **Desk** is private and cannot be shared. For work with other people, make a shared work area and invite them. Access applies to the whole area, not to each task. See [Collaboration](/help/working-together).

## Getting in

Sign in with **Continue with Google**, then open **Today** to see what needs your attention. Dana is in beta. Use **Feedback** in the sidebar to tell us what works and what does not.
