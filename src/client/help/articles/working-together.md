Shared work lives in a shared work area. Everyone you invite can read its pages, and their access level controls what they can change. Your Desk stays private.

## Access and responsibilities

Invite people from the area's Team or sharing controls. **Access** is what a person can do in Dana. A **Responsibility** is a written note about what they are expected to do. A responsibility does not give permissions; only Access does.

If you cannot edit something, your access level does not allow it. Ask someone with Full control.

## Profiles

Open **Team** and select a person to see their profile: their **Bio**, their **Access**, their **Responsibilities**, and supporting content. Use **Message** on a profile to write to them directly.

## Discussions and direct messages

Use a **Discussion** when the conversation belongs to the work. It stays on the shared area, can link to tasks, and can be resolved. Use **Direct Messages** for a private conversation between two people.
