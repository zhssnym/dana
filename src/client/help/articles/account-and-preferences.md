Open **Settings** from Dana's sidebar to manage your account and preferences.

## Account

Dana uses your Google account for sign-in. Settings shows your account details and provides **Sign out**.

## Preferences

You can choose a light or dark theme, your default Work view, timezone, and clock format.

## Download your data

Under **Data**, select **Download my data**. Dana provides a JSON file containing your account data.

Account deletion is also available in Settings and requires confirmation. Check the confirmation carefully before proceeding.
