A task can have a **Start Date**, a **Due Date**, both dates, or neither. You do not have to set both.

## Choose dates

1. Open the task.
2. Open its schedule or date control.
3. Choose a Start Date, a Due Date, or a range.
4. Save the change.

The Start Date is when the task is intended to begin. The Due Date is when it should be finished.

## Change a range in Calendar

Tasks with a date range can be adjusted in the Calendar view. Drag the appropriate end of the displayed range to change its boundary. You can also edit the dates through the task details.

## Where scheduled tasks appear

**Upcoming** shows tasks with a Start Date or a Due Date. For the way tasks become relevant to Today, see [Today and Upcoming](/help/today-and-upcoming).
