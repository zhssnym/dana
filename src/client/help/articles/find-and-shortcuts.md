Press **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, or **⌘K** on Mac, to search the tasks and pages you can open. The **Library** in the sidebar lists your pages. **Breadcrumbs** at the top show where you are; select one to go back up.

## Shortcuts

Press **?** when you are not typing to see this list in Dana. **Mod** means Ctrl on Windows and Linux, and ⌘ on Mac. Some shortcuts work only outside a text field or inside a task.

## What's new

**What's new** is a record of what has changed in Dana. It is at `/updates`, and you can read it without signing in.

- Recent updates are grouped under the date they were released.
- Each update is labeled **New**, **Improved**, or **Fixed**.
- **Earlier improvements** lists a selection of changes released before this update history began.
- Open an update to read its summary. Select **View the change on GitHub** for more detail, or **Back to all updates** to return to the list.
