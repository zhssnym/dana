Press **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, or **⌘K** on Mac, to search the tasks and pages you can open. The **Library** in the sidebar lists your pages. **Breadcrumbs** at the top show where you are; select one to go back up.

## Shortcuts

Press **?** when you are not typing to see this list in Dana. **Mod** means Ctrl on Windows and Linux, and ⌘ on Mac. Some shortcuts work only outside a text field or inside a task.

## Help & Updates

The **Help & Updates** button is in the sidebar footer, next to **Search**. Its menu has the documentation, feedback and release notes. On a phone, open the sidebar with **Menu** first.

- **Documentation** opens Dana Help.
- **Send feedback** opens the **Send feedback** dialog.
- **What's new** lists the three most recent updates and their dates. Select an update to read it, or select **View all updates** to see every update.
- **About this build** shows which version of Dana is running and when the app was built (**Client built**).

A dot on the button and a count such as "2 new" show updates you have not seen. Opening the menu marks those updates as seen on all your devices.
