Dana is in beta, and feedback helps improve the product.

## Share feedback

1. Open **Feedback** in Dana's sidebar.
2. Choose the kind of feedback you want to send.
3. Describe the problem or suggestion in plain terms.
4. Attach a screenshot when it helps explain the issue, then submit.

If something is broken, describe what you expected and what happened instead.

Feedback is not a live support chat. A submitted report does not guarantee an immediate reply.
