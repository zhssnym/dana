A page is a place to keep related work together. It can hold writing, tasks, and nested pages. Your Library helps you find and organize the pages you use.

## Desk

**Desk** is your private starting area. Use it for personal work that you do not need to share with a team.

## Shared work

You can create a separate work area and invite other people to collaborate on it. Access applies to the shared work area, not independently to each task.

## Work and Docs

Dana provides working surfaces for tasks and writing. Work focuses on tasks and progress; Docs provide space for notes and other writing. Both can carry structured content.

Use [writing and blocks](/help/writing-and-blocks) to learn what you can add to a page. To work with other people, read [Collaborate on work](/help/collaborate-on-work).
