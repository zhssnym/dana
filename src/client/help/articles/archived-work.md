Archiving and deleting are different actions.

## Archive

Archive moves work out of active views without treating it as deleted. To find archived work, open **Settings**, then **Manage archived work**.

From that page you can manage archived items and restore work you want to use again.

## Trash

Trash is for items you remove. Dana keeps trashed items for a limited period before permanent removal.

Only delete work permanently when you are sure you no longer need it. Shared work may affect other members, and Dana checks the permissions required for destructive actions.
