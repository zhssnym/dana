## A task is missing from Today

Check whether it has a future Start Date or Due Date, and whether it is still open. Today follows scheduling and assignment rules; not every task appears there automatically.

Open [Today and Upcoming](/help/today-and-upcoming) for the date behavior.

## I cannot edit shared work

Your access level may not permit the action. Check the shared work area's Team and Access settings, or ask someone with Full control.

## My change is not visible

Check that the app is connected and the save finished. If Dana displays **Update available, reload**, use the update control to load the newer application version. Reloading does not replace a failed save.

## I found a problem

Use **Feedback** in Dana to report a bug or suggest an improvement. Include what you were doing and what happened; a screenshot can help.
