These shortcuts are shared with Dana's in-app shortcut sheet. **Mod** means Ctrl on Windows/Linux and ⌘ on Mac.

Some shortcuts apply only when you are outside a text field or inside a particular task surface.

## Find the in-app list

Press **?** when you are not typing in a text field. You can also search for **Keyboard shortcuts** in Dana's search overlay.
