Open **Settings** from the sidebar. Dana uses your Google account to sign in, and **Sign out** is in Settings.

## Preferences

Choose a light or dark theme, your default Work view, your timezone, and the clock format.

## Archive and Trash

**Archive** takes work out of your active views without deleting it. Find it again in **Settings**, under **Manage archived work**. **Trash** holds removed items for a limited time before Dana deletes them permanently.

## Your data

Under **Data**, select **Download my data** to get a JSON file of your account data. You can also delete your account in Settings. Read the confirmation carefully, because shared work can affect other people.

## When something looks wrong

If a change does not appear, check that the save finished. If Dana shows **Update available, reload**, select it to load the new version. To report a bug or an idea, use **Feedback** in the sidebar. Say what you did and what happened, and add a screenshot if it helps. Feedback is not a live chat, so a reply can take time.
