Dana keeps tasks, writing, and discussions close to the work they belong to.

## Sign in

1. Open Dana and select **Continue with Google**.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Open **Today** to see tasks that need your attention.

Dana is currently in beta. You can share feedback through the app.

## Find your Desk

Your Desk is your private starting area. It provides space for your own tasks and documents. Other people do not automatically have access to your Desk.

You can also create separate shared work areas when you want to collaborate.
