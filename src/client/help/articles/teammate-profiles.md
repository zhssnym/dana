A teammate profile brings relevant information about a person together with their role in shared work.

## Open a profile

Open **Team** in a shared work area, then select a person. Depending on the surface, Dana may show the profile in a side panel or on a full page.

## What a profile contains

- **Bio** — A short introduction controlled by the person.
- **Access** — What the member can do in the shared work area.
- **Responsibilities** — Their assigned role or expectations, recorded separately from Access.
- **Content** — Supporting writing and files visible according to the shared area's existing access rules.

Changing written responsibilities does not grant permissions. Use the actual Access controls to change what someone can edit.

Profiles may also provide **Message** to start a direct conversation.
