Dana provides task lists that bring work together from different pages.

## Today

**Today** helps you focus on the work that needs attention now. Dana uses a task's Start Date when it has one, or its Due Date when there is no Start Date, to decide when scheduled work becomes active.

You can also pull a task into Today yourself.

## Upcoming

**Upcoming** shows scheduled work. A task appears here when it has a Start Date, a Due Date, or both.

## Assigned

**Assigned** helps you see tasks and who is responsible for them.

Other focus lists can include overdue work and tasks with no date. Your available lists and their placement depend on your sidebar settings.
