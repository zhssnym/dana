### 4.24 labels

The M1 Label catalog: the definitions, in two scopes and no third. A
**Personal** Label is owned by one account and private to it; a **Folder** Label
belongs to one Folder and is shared with its members. A Library scope spanning
several Folders is M2+, and a Personal Label applied to a Folder remains a
Personal Label rather than becoming a scope of its own.

**Scope is part of identity.** Personal "Research", Folder A "Research" and
Folder B "Research" are three Labels with three ids and are never merged by name.

Columns, constraints and indexes: `app/db/migrations/0022_labels.sql`.
`archived_at` is the archive state and is non-destructive.

**The two composite uniques, `labels(id, owner_user_id)` and
`labels(folder_id, id)`, exist to be referenced, not queried.** They are the
targets of the assignment tables' composite foreign keys (4.25 and 4.26, and
4.27 respectively), which make a mis-scoped assignment unrepresentable. Do not
drop them because nothing selects through them.

**`labels_ownership_check` is spelled so it can never evaluate to UNKNOWN.** A
CHECK passes on UNKNOWN, so the obvious both-or-neither spellings over nullable
columns admit exactly the row they were written to refuse; every operand here is
an `IS NULL` or an equality over a NOT NULL column. The constraint catalog sees
only names and columns, never whether an expression has teeth.

**Case-insensitive name uniqueness is per catalog and includes archived rows**:
an archived "Research" still holds the name, and a person wanting it back
restores or renames that Label. The two unique indexes are partial because the
other scope's key column is NULL on every row of the opposite catalog.

**The cascades are the account and Folder lifecycles and nothing else.**
Personal Labels go with their account (8.10); a Folder's Labels go with the
Folder when it is purged. Soft-deleting a Folder touches no row here.
