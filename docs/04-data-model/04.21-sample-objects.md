### 4.21 sample_objects

The register of what the sample workspace wrote into an account at its first
sign-in (7.1, 7.20). Columns and constraints:
`app/db/migrations/0007_sample_objects.sql`.

- `id` is the object's own id and **deliberately has no foreign key**: the
  object keeps its id through every edit the person makes and removal must still
  find it. A row whose object the person destroyed themselves names nothing and
  is dropped by the next removal.
- Objects that ride another's cascade (steps, blocks, comments, reminders,
  pulls, events) are not registered.
- No index: the primary key serves the only read, one person's register.
