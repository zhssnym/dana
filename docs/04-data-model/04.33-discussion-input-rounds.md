### 4.33 discussion_input_rounds, discussion_input_options, discussion_input_responses

Structured input inside a Discussion (issue #321, "Ask for input"). Columns, constraints and
indexes: `app/db/migrations/0030_consultation_input_rounds.sql`.

**`discussion_input_rounds`**: one round of one Discussion. `mode` is exactly
`choose | advice | concerns`; these are response shapes, not Discussion types. `state` is
`open | closed`, and a closed round never reopens. `close_reason` is `closed` (a person closed it,
`closed_by`) or `discussion_resolved` (resolving the Discussion closed it in the same transaction,
and `closed_by` is null). `discussion_input_rounds_closure_check` ties `state`, `closed_at` and
`close_reason` together. **At most one open round per Discussion** is the partial unique index
`discussion_input_rounds_one_open_key` on `(discussion_id) WHERE state = 'open'`.

**`discussion_input_options`**: the explicit options of a `choose` round, written when it opens
and never edited, ordered by `position`.

**`discussion_input_responses`**: one current response per participant per round
(`discussion_input_responses_round_id_user_id_key`). A change is an update in place and a
withdrawal deletes the row; neither is admitted once the round is closed, which is the freeze.
The row carries its round's `mode` under a composite key against `(id, mode)`, so
`discussion_input_responses_shape_check` holds each mode to its own columns: choose has exactly
`option_id` (an option of its own round, by a composite key on `(round_id, option_id)`), advice
has a non-blank `body`, concerns has `concern` (`no_concern | concern`) and `concern` requires a
non-blank `body` as its reason. `body` is at most 4000 characters.

Nothing here is a weight, score, ranking, veto or winner. Every key to a round or Discussion
cascades, so purging a Discussion or its Folder takes its rounds, options and responses. The
three users keys (`created_by`, `closed_by`, `user_id`) are RESTRICT and outlive the account
(8.10). Rounds are not copied by Folder duplication or workflows, and are not Dana Link targets.
