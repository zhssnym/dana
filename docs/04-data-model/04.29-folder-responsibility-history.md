### 4.29 folder_responsibility_history

The append-only record behind 4.28: every committed revision of a
Responsibility document, every ending of a standing, and both directions of the
self-edit grant. **Nothing in this table is ever updated or deleted by the
application**, and no route in section 7 does either. Columns, constraints and
indexes: `app/db/migrations/0025_responsibility_document.sql`.

- `member_id` is the subject; `actor_id` is who did it; `content` is the
  snapshot as committed and is never rewritten.
- **`folder_responsibility_history_revision_check` is the shape rule**: an entry
  carrying content carries a revision number and a hash and is a creation or an
  update; an entry carrying none of the three is an ending or a grant toggle.
  It is written as biconditionals over never-UNKNOWN operands because one table
  holds both shapes and a CHECK passes on UNKNOWN (the hazard 4.24 records).
- Only grant toggles may lack a `responsibility_id`.
- **The partial unique index on (`responsibility_id`, `revision_no`) makes a
  revision immutable in the database**: a correction must arrive as the next
  revision, and the earlier text stays as its author left it.
- **Grant toggles live here rather than in 4.13's events** because a grant is
  permission to write one Responsibility; beside the revisions it makes
  possible, "who let them" and "what they then wrote" read as one story ordered
  by `effective_at`.
