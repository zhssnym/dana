### 4.1 users

Columns and constraints: `app/db/migrations/0000_core_schema.sql`.

- `google_sub` is the OpenID Connect subject claim, the stable Google identity; `email` is
  for display and invite matching only. Email is stored lowercased and is unique among
  active accounts only.
- `timezone` must validate against the IANA tzdb at write time (4.11).
- Deletion is soft: `deleted_at` is set and the identity is scrubbed (`google_sub`,
  `email`, `display_name`, `avatar_url` set null), which is the only way those columns are
  null. Soft deletion keeps `created_by` and `completed_by` references valid on surviving
  tasks. A null `display_name` renders as "Deleted user".
