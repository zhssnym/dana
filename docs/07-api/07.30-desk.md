### 7.30 Desk

Desk is the account's default, single-member project. Its project root is **Work** and a new
account also receives one Doc tab named **Docs**. Additional Desk tabs are documentation
pages: `POST /folders/:id/tabs` accepts `kind: "doc"` for a Desk and answers 422 for Work,
Discussion or the usual-project choice. Doc tabs otherwise use the ordinary page rename,
reorder and Trash operations.

Desk has no Discussion surface. `POST /folders/:id/discussions` answers 409 when `:id`
belongs to Desk, and `POST /tasks/:id/discussion` answers 409 for a Desk Task. Existing
Discussion records are not destroyed merely because their Folder is a Desk; their ordinary
read, Trash, restore and purge rules remain the data-lifecycle authority. The product does
not create or surface new Desk Discussions.
