How this supports fast scanning without feeling busy

Scanning speed comes from making the task title the only 14px/400 element on the row and pushing everything else down to 12px metadata or into quiet chips, so the eye ladders titles without reading anything else. Weight, not size, carries urgency: overdue and emphasized rows step to 500, which the eye catches in peripheral vision without adding height or color noise.

Restraint comes from role separation. Nothing competes with the content, which is what makes 44px targets and a compact 4px rhythm coexist.
