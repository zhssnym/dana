### 8.9 Ownership transfer

One deterministic rule, two callers (owner leave; account deletion): the new owner is the
remaining member with the earliest folder_members.created_at, ties broken by lower user
id. Update folders.owner_id on every page of the project (step 8: a page's owner is its
project's), keep the new owner's membership row unchanged (role stays
'member'; ownership is the folder column, one source of truth). Default folders never
transfer (INV-2). Implementation: `transferOwnership` in
`app/src/server/domain/folders.ts`.
