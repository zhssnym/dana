### 8.2 Today evaluation

Implementation: `myDayView` in `app/src/server/views/my-day.ts`, with `qualifyDate` and
`myDayGroup` in `app/src/server/time/predicates.ts`.

- A task is on V's Today page when it is pending, in a folder V can access, and either
  assigned to V with an activation date (8.1) on or before today(V), or pulled by V (a
  my_day_entries row for V).
- Its qualify_date is the earlier of the automatic date (the activation date, when the
  automatic clause holds) and the pulled date (the entry's added_on, when an entry exists).
- **Carried** holds rows whose qualify_date < today(V); **Today** holds rows whose
  qualify_date = today(V); the recap is Q1R (section 6). Orderings are Q1's.

Consequences, each intended: nothing ever leaves this page by time passing (snowball); a
pull is one decision, not a daily ritual; pulling a future or someone else's task shows it
with its assignee chip; completing from anywhere surfaces it in the recap; abandoning
removes it from the open groups instantly and keeps it out of the recap.

The automatic clause holds on one date, the activation date, never on an OR of start and
due clauses, which would give one row two candidate dates and two answers to which group it
lands in. So a task starting today and due in ten days is in **Today** the day it starts
and in **Carried** after; Carried means work that began before today and is not finished,
not lateness, which stays 8.1's relation to the deadline. A Start-only task behaves the
same way and never becomes overdue. A task carrying neither date never qualifies
automatically, because the clause holds on a date the task carries and age is not one.
