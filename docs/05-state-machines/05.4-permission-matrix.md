### 5.4 Permission matrix (authorization, not state)

The matrix is `RULES` in `app/src/shared/folder-actions.ts`: every folder action, the least
Access preset that may perform it, and the narrow grants beside that bar; `mayPerform` there
is the one resolver both the routes and the client call. The rules it does not carry:

- **Server-enforced on every endpoint.** The client may hide a control it computes from the
  same table; nothing the client computes permits anything.
- **A non-member gets 404, never 403**, so a folder's existence is not confirmed to anyone
  outside it (10.2). A member below the bar gets 403.
- **Ownership is custody, not authority.** Ordinary work and member administration follow
  the member's Access; the owner alone transfers ownership, permanently destroys the
  folder (with Full control as well), and restores their own Access; the owner may restore
  a trashed folder whatever their Access. The owner cannot be removed by anyone.
- **Assignment-bounded grants read the assignee in the writing transaction**, under the row
  lock the mutation takes; a projection saying "assigned to me" never authorizes.
- **The default folder is never shared or deleted** (409, INV-2), whoever asks.
