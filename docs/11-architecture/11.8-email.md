### 11.8 Email

Resend is the one provider for every template, behind a notification module that logs
instead of sending in development and tests; the 11.10 construction seam's
options.notifications sink, when injected, replaces that logging so tests can observe
sends. The 10.7 deliverability checklist applies unchanged. The free tier covers v1; its
daily cap is the number to watch if reminder volume grows. Templates:
`app/src/server/notify/index.ts`.

A folder invitation (7.5 with a folder_id) and a bare connection invitation are separate
templates, because they differ in subject, heading, context and icon and the template name
is what the sink reads: under one name no test could tell them apart. The template is
chosen at the call site from the invite row already in hand rather than by a second read.
