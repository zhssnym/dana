### 4.14 notifications

The fan-out of an event to the people it concerns: the assignee of an assignment, the
participants of a thread, the members of a deleted shared folder. Derived rows; deleting
an event does not retract its notifications.

Columns and constraints: `app/db/migrations/0003_v2_expand.sql`.

`user_id` is the recipient; a null `read_at` is unread. UNIQUE (user_id, event_id) makes
fan-out idempotent, so a tick replayed after a crash lands on the constraint (11.7). An
actor is never notified of their own act.
