### 4.23 task_dependencies

One directed relation between two tasks: the **dependent** depends on the
**blocking** one. `blocks` is the same row read from the other end and is never
a mirrored second row.

Columns, constraints and index: `app/db/migrations/0019_task_dependencies.sql`
(one edge per ordered pair, no self-edge).

**No derived state is stored.** Whether a dependency is active, satisfied or
unresolved is a function of the blocking task's status at read time: pending
means active, completed means satisfied, abandoned means unresolved and **not**
satisfied, and reopening a completed blocker makes it active again. A persisted
`blocked` status would be a second answer to a question the `tasks` row already
answers, and the one that goes stale.

**Both endpoint cascades are the trash rule.** A soft-deleted task keeps its
edges and restores whole; only the permanent delete of 7.13 and the 11.7 purge
take them.

`created_by` is RESTRICT: accounts are scrubbed (8.10), never deleted, so the
restriction never fires and makes that assumption explicit.

**The row carries a surrogate `id` although its endpoints name it uniquely**,
because an edge must be removable and one endpoint may be a task the caller must
not learn anything about (7.25); addressing a deletion by the pair would hand
back the withheld id.
