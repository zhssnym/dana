### 4.3 folders

Columns and constraints: `app/db/migrations/0000_core_schema.sql`, `0003_v2_expand.sql`
(identity columns, `deleted_at`), `0006_folder_cover_tiers.sql` (cover preset and crop) and
`0061_projects_and_tabs.sql` (projects and tabs).

- **A project is its root row** (`id = project_id`): it keeps the name, icon and cover, and it
  is always one of its own tabs. Every other page carries its project's id, and the page tree
  (`parent_folder_id`) never leaves a project; a move is one UPDATE, and the subtree's
  `project_id` follows by the key's ON UPDATE CASCADE. Destroying a project root destroys its
  pages; a page that survives it (one its destroyer could not destroy) becomes a project of
  its own first, as a surviving child always became a root.
- **A tab is a root-level page of a project with a `tab_position`**, the project's tab order
  (a fractional key in C collation). A sub-page has none and never has tabs.
- **`kind` says what a page is for**: `doc` (writing, which may hold tasks and pages), `work`
  (tasks and the words around them) or `discussion` (the project's threads; always a tab, and
  one live per project). Only a new project's root may have none, until its picker is answered
  (7.4). Desk is a Work project.

- **One default folder per account (INV-2)**, held by the unique index on `owner_id` where
  `is_default`. `is_default` is the record; the default folder's name is an ordinary value
  in `name` (Desk; a v1 account may keep an older name, section 2).
- **Ownership is read from `owner_id` only**; the membership role on 4.4 never encodes it.
  `ON DELETE RESTRICT` on `owner_id` is a tripwire: the account deletion transaction must
  transfer or delete owned folders first, and if it fails to, the database refuses rather
  than corrupting.
- **The identity columns (`icon`, `color`, cover, `description`) are the folder's, not a
  member's**: one look for every member, any member may change it, and the event log of
  4.13 records who. A member's personal shelf and color on 4.4 remain per-viewer. `icon` is
  a key from the server-known icon set.
- **A cover has at most one source**: an uploaded file or a preset, never both, and no
  cover at all is legal (the file FK's SET NULL produces it). `cover_focal_y` is the
  vertical crop, 0 to 100. This mirrors 4.20 so a folder can wear every cover tier a place
  page can; it differs from 4.20 only in admitting no cover.
- **`deleted_at` marks Trash**; the 11.7 pass purges at 30 days. Every read excludes
  trashed rows except through the Trash surface of 7.13, and every query in section 6
  carries the implicit `deleted_at IS NULL` guard.
