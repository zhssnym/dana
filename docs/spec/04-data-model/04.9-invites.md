### 4.9 invites

Columns and constraints: `app/db/migrations/0000_core_schema.sql`,
`0023_folder_access.sql` (`access`) and `0025_responsibility_document.sql`
(`responsibility`).

- **INV-11** is the partial unique index on (inviter_id, email, folder or none) where
  status is pending. The pending index on `email` serves the at-login lookup (Q12); the
  index on `inviter_id` serves the sent list.
- `last_sent_at` is the resend throttle anchor; `responded_at` is set on any terminal
  transition; `accepted_by` is the account that accepted.
- `task_id` records assignment intent applied at acceptance (8.7), and requires a folder.
- A deleted folder cascades its pending invites away, which is the required invalidation
  expressed as removal. `invalidated` is the status used when the inviter's account is
  deleted.
- `access` is the Access preset a folder invite grants on acceptance (7.4); it is present
  exactly when a folder is.
- **`responsibility` is parked content, not a document.** An invitation names an email
  address that may have no account behind it, so there is nobody to hold a 4.28 row for.
  It becomes a document with its first revision at acceptance, whose actor is the inviter
  who chose the text. An invitation that never becomes a membership never becomes a
  document, and the content goes with the row. It requires a folder: a Responsibility is a
  fact about a membership of a folder, so without one it would state nothing, and the route
  refuses it with 422.
