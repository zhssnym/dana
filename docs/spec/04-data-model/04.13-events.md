### 4.13 events

One row per recorded act, written at the section 10.2 authorization chokepoint by the
mutation that passed it. Read by history surfaces and fanned out to notifications.
Retention: 90 days rolling, purged by the section 11.7 tick.

Columns and constraints: `app/db/migrations/0003_v2_expand.sql`.

- `subject_id` has no FK: the subject may be purged before the event.
- `subject_name` is the name at act time, so a line still reads after a rename.
- `folder_id` is the visibility scope. `detail` is the half a verb cannot carry.
- `actor_id` is `ON DELETE RESTRICT` because users are soft-deleted.

Visibility follows the subject: an event is readable by whoever could read its folder
scope at read time, enforced at 10.2 like every read.
