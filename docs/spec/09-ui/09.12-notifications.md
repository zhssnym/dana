### 9.12 Notifications

The bell (`app/src/client/components/notifications-bell.tsx`) sits in the sidebar footer row
with a monochrome unread badge, capped at "9+". It opens the viewer's notifications (4.14),
newest first, one sentence each, read for the recipient; unread is told by tone and a dot,
never by hue, and no more than the last 30 days are rendered.

A notification is a pointer, not a task: pressing a row marks it read and opens its subject
— a task at /app/tasks/:id, a folder at its page, a post at its thread (a task's on its
Discussion tab) scrolled to the post — and rows carry no per-row actions. Posts in one
thread fold into one row per read state ("Bilal and Sara posted · 3 new", then
"in "Launch" · “the words”" on the quieter second line), which opens the thread where its
unread posts start and marks them all read; mentions never fold ("Bilal mentioned you").
The list is live while the app is open: it re-reads about every twenty seconds while the tab
is visible, and again whenever the bell is opened. "Mark all read" shows while any row is unread and is the surface's only
other verb; nothing is deleted by hand, rows ageing out with 4.13's retention. Invites
never appear here; the invite rows above the page (9.8) are theirs.

A comment on a task reaches the task's assignee and creator as well as the thread's earlier
voices. An actor is never notified of their own act (4.14).
