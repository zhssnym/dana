### 9.12 Notifications

The bell (`app/src/client/components/notifications-bell.tsx`) sits in the sidebar header
with a monochrome unread badge, capped at "9+". It opens the viewer's notifications (4.14),
newest first, one sentence each, read for the recipient; unread is told by tone and a dot,
never by hue, and no more than the last 30 days are rendered.

A notification is a pointer, not a task: pressing a row marks it read and opens its subject
— a task in the viewer's preferred surface via /app/tasks/:id, a folder at its page, a
comment at the task or folder holding it with the conversation open — and rows carry no
per-row actions. "Mark all read" shows while any row is unread and is the surface's only
other verb; nothing is deleted by hand, rows ageing out with 4.13's retention. Invites
never appear here; the gate of 9.8 is theirs.

A comment on a task reaches the task's assignee and creator as well as the thread's earlier
voices. An actor is never notified of their own act (4.14).
