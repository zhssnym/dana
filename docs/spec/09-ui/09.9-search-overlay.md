### 9.9 Search overlay

Opened by the sidebar's search control or shortcut (the control is 9.1's "Search"; the
overlay itself is a dialog named "Search Dana", the prototype's own, which 9.1's names
table does not reach — item 57, A2); input with 300 ms debounce; before anything is typed
it offers the pages of 9.1's block as rows to go to, the prototype's own reason being that
they are few and fixed and cost one glance, where every folder and shelf the viewer has
would be the sidebar drawn twice. Results group by kind: tasks (matching title, notes, and
step titles), folders and shelves (name and description), and pages (matching a page's own
prose — sections, paragraphs, captions). Task results keep the Pending, Abandoned,
Completed order of Q8, each row wearing its status as a badge rather than standing under a
status heading, because two classification schemes as peers is the fault DES-152 took out
of the sidebar; within a kind a title match ranks above a body match. Enter opens the
first result; a row opens its thing in place. Footer: "Showing matches from every folder
you can access." (Amended toward `prototype-v2` at RFC 0005 change control item 55, and at
the polish round of 2026-08-30, search F5, F7 and F8: the pages are matched as they are
typed as well as offered before anything is, so a place is findable by its name or by its
question; Pending is announced rather than drawn, the badge being worn by Completed and
Abandoned alone, a chip that says "ordinary" on nearly every row saying nothing; and the
overlay has one close control, the "×", the Esc key having moved from a cap in the header
that read as a button into the footer's hints beside the arrows.)
