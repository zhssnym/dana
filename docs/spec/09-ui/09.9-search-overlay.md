### 9.9 Search overlay

Opened by 9.1's "Search" control or its shortcut; the overlay is a dialog named "Search
Dana" (`app/src/client/components/search-overlay.tsx`). Input is debounced. Before
anything is typed it offers the pages of 9.1's page block as rows to go to — they are few
and fixed, where every folder and shelf would be the sidebar drawn twice — and as the
viewer types, pages are matched too, by name or by question.

Results group by kind: tasks (title, notes, step titles), folders and shelves (name and
description), and pages (a page's own prose). Task results keep Q8's Pending, Abandoned,
Completed order, each row wearing its status as a badge rather than standing under a
status heading, since two classification schemes as peers is a fault; Pending is announced
but wears no visible badge, a chip saying "ordinary" on nearly every row saying nothing. Within a kind a title
match ranks above a body match. Enter opens the first result; a row opens its thing in
place. The footer says matches come from every folder the viewer can access. The overlay
has one close control; Escape is a hint, not a button.
