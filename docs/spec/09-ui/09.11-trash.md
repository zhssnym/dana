### 9.11 Trash

One page listing every deleted task and folder the viewer can still reach, inside the 30-day window of 4.3/4.5, newest first, each row naming what it was, where it lived, who deleted it and when (from 4.13). Restore puts the thing back where it was as exactly one row; a restored folder returns with its membership intact. Anyone with access to the object may restore it — Trash is scoped by access, not by who deleted. The page states the rule once, under its heading: "Deleted items are kept for 30 days, then removed."; each row's own share of the window reads "30d left". Its tab is named "Trash" and wears the page's mark, and a row's Restore and delete are drawn at rest rather than on hover. (Amended at the polish round of 2026-08-30: the rule had two wordings, one for a full page and one for an empty one; the tab was a blank with a close cross; and a row whose only controls appear on hover is a row with nothing to press.) Empty state: "Nothing deleted", with the line "Tasks and folders you delete land here first, so a wrong press costs a click rather than the thing itself." No capture anywhere on the page. Each row whose folder the viewer owns also offers a permanent delete, "Delete for ever" behind a confirm that names the thing and says the act cannot be undone, and the page offers "Empty trash" over those rows while it holds any; a member who does not own the folder sees Restore alone (7.13 as amended by RFC 0005 change control items 36, 38 and 53; built at S28b, item 56). (Copy amended toward `prototype-v2` at RFC 0005 change control item 55.)
