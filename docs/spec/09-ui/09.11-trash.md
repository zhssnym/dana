### 9.11 Trash

One page (`app/src/client/screens/trash-screen.tsx`) listing every deleted task and folder
the viewer can still reach inside the 30-day window of 4.3/4.5, newest first, each row
one line naming what it was and where it lived, with who deleted it and when as the
row's tooltip (4.13). Restore puts the thing
back where it was as exactly one row; a restored folder returns with its membership
intact. Anyone with access to the object may restore it: Trash is scoped by access, not by
who deleted.

Each row states the retention rule as its own remaining share of the window ("30 days
left"); the page does not repeat it. Empty, the page says "Nothing in Trash." as one muted
line where the first row would be. A row's controls take the place of its days left,
in the same slot, on hover or keyboard focus, and always on a touchscreen, which has no
hover. No capture anywhere on the page.

A row whose page the viewer owns also offers "Delete forever" behind a confirmation
that names the thing and says the act cannot be undone, and the page offers "Empty trash"
over those rows while it holds any; both are drawn in the danger style and the
confirmation's other control is "Cancel". A member who does not own the page sees Restore
alone (7.13).
