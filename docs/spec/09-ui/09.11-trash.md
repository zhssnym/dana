### 9.11 Trash

One page (`app/src/client/screens/trash-screen.tsx`) listing every deleted task and folder
the viewer can still reach inside the 30-day window of 4.3/4.5, newest first, each row
naming what it was, where it lived, who deleted it and when (4.13). Restore puts the thing
back where it was as exactly one row; a restored folder returns with its membership
intact. Anyone with access to the object may restore it: Trash is scoped by access, not by
who deleted.

The page states the retention rule once, under its heading, whether or not it holds
anything, and each row shows its own remaining share of the window. A row's controls are
drawn at rest, not on hover, because a touchscreen has no hover. No capture anywhere on
the page.

A row whose folder the viewer owns also offers a permanent delete behind a confirmation
that names the thing and says the act cannot be undone, and the page offers "Empty trash"
over those rows while it holds any; a member who does not own the folder sees Restore
alone (7.13).
