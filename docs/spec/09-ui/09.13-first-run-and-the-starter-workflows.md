### 9.13 First run and the starter workflows

**The sample workspace.** 7.1 writes 7.20's sample workspace in the provisioning
transaction, so the first account a person sees is full. In Today's top bar, while the sample
is present, stands a strip (`app/src/client/onboarding/SampleStrip.tsx`, words in
`app/src/client/onboarding/sample.ts`): one line, "Sample workspace · Remove · Keep it". Remove is
the same act as Remove on 9.7's Data row "Sample workspace" (offered twice because the strip is the
moment a person wants it): one press removes through DELETE /sample, with no confirm, and offers the
undo bar, whose inverse is POST /sample; a sample folder left standing because it is
shared, or moved to Trash because the person wrote in it, is said in a toast. No confirm is
needed because removal destroys only what the sample wrote; the undo writes the sample afresh. "Keep it" dismisses the strip on this device only; 9.7's row
is the way back. The strip stands on Today alone, in the bar rather than above the title so the title
stands where it stands on every screen.

**The starter offer.** In place of Today's empty state, where a new person lands, and
under Today's "New task" (the person's own first act comes first), a fresh account is offered the starter
workflows (`app/src/client/onboarding/StarterOffer.tsx`, `app/src/client/onboarding/starters.ts`) as a heading, one plain row per
starter and "No thanks": bundled workflow files
(7.16) that import with one press, each a worked example of a real piece of work rather
than a tour of the interface. Importing one teaches where things live by putting something
in each place — a folder in the Library, a page with sections, tasks reaching the views of
9.4 and 9.5 — rather than by describing it.

**Fresh** means the account holds no folder but Desk and no task anywhere, which the client
already knows from what it read to draw the page, so the offer needs no endpoint. It stops
being drawn the moment either is false, including the moment an import lands, and does not
return. While the sample workspace stands the offer is therefore not drawn; removing the
sample answers the offer as "No thanks" does, on that device: a person who has just said no
to example content is not offered more of it. The sample itself does not seed Trash.

**Each starter is an ordinary import** through POST /api/v1/workflows/import (7.16), with
the same validation as a person's own file. Nothing is seeded or special-cased, and no row
is created that a person could not have created, so a starter cannot drift from the import.

**A starter may contain only what a workflow file may** (`app/src/shared/workflows.ts`),
and it is right that the format has no key for comments or reminders; a later widening of
the format must not take these two with it. A comment must be attributed to somebody, and
a fabricated one would be the product inventing a colleague. A reminder would email a plan
the person did not make, at an hour they did not choose, most likely to someone who never
came back.

**Every starter says on its own page that it can be deleted**, as the first paragraph block
of its folder, because the offer's copy is gone the moment it is answered and the page is
what the person still has.

The starter files are the design record's. What they must be is worked examples of
problems a person actually has, each solved the way Dana solves it: a starter that
demonstrates the interface teaches how to click and not why to return.
