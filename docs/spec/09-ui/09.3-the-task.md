### 9.3 The task (create and edit are one artifact, and the artifact is the task)

There is no task form. A task is its row and its page. Clicking a task anywhere (a list
row, a canvas task block, a board card, the calendar, search, a dependency link) opens its
page in the way Settings > Appearance > "Open tasks in" chooses, stored as 4.19's
`user_settings.task_open`:

- **Center peek** (`dialog`, the default): the page in a centered sheet about 960px wide
  over the dimmed page; a press on the dimmed page closes it. The scrim fades and the
  sheet fades and rises in on the motion tokens, and eases out before it closes.
- **Side peek** (`panel`): a 480px panel at the right edge that makes the
  page narrower rather than covering it, so the list stays usable and clicking another
  row swaps the peek's task.
- **Full page** (`tab`): /app/tasks/:id as a page inside the app's shell and sidebar.

Only tasks open in a peek: a sub-page (a `/page` block, a sidebar row) is a place and
navigates, as Notion's sub-page links do. A peek's header keeps its left for the
breadcrumb and carries ↑/↓ (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K and J) to step to the previous and next
task in the order the page under it draws them; they are absent when the task was opened
from somewhere that lists none (search, the calendar).

A phone always opens the full page. An open peek is `?task=<id>` on the current page, so
it survives a reload and Back closes it; Escape and the close control close it too, and
"Open as full page" turns it into /app/tasks/:id. /app/tasks/:id is the shareable address
and is always the full page. Every edit commits as it is made — no Save control, no draft
state — and 9.1's undo bar covers the destructive ones. An unsaved task has no page: a
task is made inline and then opened.

The page is `app/src/client/components/task-surface.tsx` over
`app/src/client/components/task-card.tsx`, the same in all three. Its order: the title
(large, renamed in place), one properties block, then the page's two tabs **Work** (the
task's document, the Task composer, where the checkbox is a subtask: `/` Subtask or `[]` + space turns the line into one of the task's subtasks, or on an empty line opens "Type a subtask", and a pasted checklist joins the subtasks in order, ticks kept, nested items flattened, while its other writing stays; over the 50-subtask cap it stays as pasted) and **Discussion** (its comments, never on the body; read like a page's thread: opening it marks it read for the person on the server and clears its bell rows, it lands at the first unread post under a "New" hairline, and chosen with a pointer it puts the cursor in its reply box).
The task's creation and completion from 4.13 (actor, viewer-local time, "Deleted user"
fallback) are read in History, not drawn on the page. The properties block is a FIXED set of quiet rows — Status first, then
Date, Reminder, Repeat, Assignee, Labels — each but Status shown only
when set, with Steps, Dependencies and Attachments under it only when the task has some,
plus one "Add property" menu that offers the unset ones (and Steps, Dependency and
Attachment) and reveals the chosen row already asking its question. There are no custom
properties. **Status** is Not started, In progress, Done or Abandoned, the first three by the Board's own rule
(`boardStatusOf`, so the two never disagree): a pending task whose start date is today or
earlier is In progress. Choosing In progress sets the start date to today (or the due date
when that is earlier), Not started clears it, and Done is 7.6's complete; an abandoned
task reads Abandoned and any other choice restores it. Nothing on the page is dragged or collapsed.
The page's one "More actions" menu holds History, Copy link, Duplicate and Delete task;
completing, abandoning and restoring are the Status row's, and the breadcrumb names the page
the task lives in, so neither is repeated there. A task is moved from its row's "More actions".
Comments are plain text under their author, and the comment composer attaches no files: a
task's files live in its Attachments section. Pickers open on the answer, not the
instrument — resolved presets first, a calendar or list behind them.

Field rules (limits and their messages: `app/src/shared/tasks.ts`,
`app/src/client/components/task-copy.ts`; a refused value answers 422 with the field
named):

- **Title** is required; an empty commit is refused, not saved.
- **Due date** is a day. The due picker's Time field writes 4.8's reminder on that day,
  never a due_time; a stored v1 due_time is displayed and clearable, never settable.
- **The Date row is the Schedule**: a Start Date, a Due Date, both, or neither. Its picker
  switches between **Due** and **Starts**; presets, the grid and Clear answer for the end
  chosen, and the grid shades the span. A start after the due date (or a due date before
  the start) is refused in the picker with "The start must be on or before the due date."
  and neither end moves. Clearing the start cascades nothing. The row reads "Starts 24 Sep",
  "24 Sep – 30 Sep", or the due date alone (a same-day pair is one day); overdue is the due
  date's alone. A task not yet created takes only a due date.
- **Reminder** and **repeat** require a due date: "Add property" offers them only once the
  task has one.
- **Repeat** is preset-driven (daily, weekdays, weekly on chosen days, every N days or
  weeks, monthly on day 1 to 28, yearly, optional end date); never a raw RRULE box.
- **Assignee** offers the target folder's members, "Invite by email", and "Unassigned"
  last after a separator; hidden in single-member folders (INV-5).
- **Steps** add, check, rename in place, reorder by drag or keyboard; a step's own "…"
  holds "Make this a task" and "Remove".
- **Attachments** are files and links per 4.16 and 10.3; a pasted URL becomes a link,
  never a fake file.

Clearing the date keeps 8.4's warning verbatim, and moving a task warns when an assignee
would be unassigned (words: `app/src/client/components/task-copy.ts`). "Delete task" moves the task to Trash at once, with no confirmation, and offers the undo bar; a task's block menu on a page does the same.

Rows (`app/src/client/components/task-row.tsx`):

- A row carries its section after its folder chip, read as a path, and omits it where the
  row already sits under that section's heading.
- A row is one line, 32–36px: checkbox, title, then muted meta on the right (date,
  or the Schedule as "Starts Mon" / "Mon – 30 Sep", folder only where the list spans folders, assignee face, steps n/m, repeat glyph) and
  the star, drawn on hover and whenever it is set. The meta is text, not controls.
- One model turns a task's fields into words for row and card alike
  (`app/src/client/tasks/task-model.ts`). Wherever a person is drawn, their own uploaded
  picture (7.15) is drawn; otherwise their default face, a small drawn character chosen
  deterministically from their name, so one person looks the same everywhere. The account
  picture stored from Google stays stored but is not drawn, and no surface draws initials.
- Pressing anywhere on a row opens the task; the checkbox and the star are the row's only
  controls. There is no chevron, no row overflow menu and no rename in the row: the task's
  panel carries its title and its one "…" menu. Shift-click or Cmd/Ctrl-click selects
  rows, and a selection shows a bar whose acts are words: Complete, Move, Date, Delete,
  Clear. On a touch screen the star is an indicator only, never a tap target.
- An empty block draws the control that fills it rather than nothing.

Entry defaults, derived from the page's own spine so a made task appears where it was made:

| Opened from | defaults |
| - | - |
| Today | folder Desk, a Today entry (membership), dated today |
| Scheduled list | folder Desk, dated today |
| A calendar cell (its inline "New task" line) | folder Desk, dated that cell's day |
| Assignments | folder Desk, assigned to the viewer |
| A folder page, its loose block | that folder |
| A section's capture | that folder, that section |
| A board column | that column's value, whatever its axis — folder, person, day, or section |

Boards (`app/src/client/components/task-board.tsx`): a section may carry a mark (a folder's glyph or a
person's face) inline in its heading; Overdue refuses a drop, being a relation to today
rather than a value.
