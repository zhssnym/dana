### 9.3 The task (create and edit are one artifact, and the artifact is the task)

There is no task form. A task is its row and its panel, and a task opens in exactly one
place: on a desktop, a side panel at the right edge (480px) that makes the page narrower
rather than covering it, so the list stays visible and usable and clicking another row
swaps the panel's task; on a phone, the same view as the whole page. An open panel is
`?task=<id>` on the current page, so it survives a reload and Back closes it; Escape and
the close control close it too. /app/tasks/:id is the shareable address: on a desktop it
opens the panel over the task's folder, on a phone it is the task's page. There is no
dialog, no "Open in" choice, no new-tab arm and no inline expansion. Every edit commits as
it is made — no Save control, no draft state — and 9.1's undo bar covers the destructive
ones. An unsaved task has no panel: a task is made inline and then opened.

4.19's `user_settings.task_open` is no longer read; the stored value and its browser mirror
are kept untouched.

The panel is `app/src/client/components/task-surface.tsx` over
`app/src/client/components/task-card.tsx`. Its order: the title (large, renamed in place),
one properties block, the notes (the Task composer), Steps, Attachments and Dependencies
only when the task has some, Comments, then the task's creation and completion from 4.13
(actor, viewer-local time, "Deleted user" fallback). The properties block is quiet rows —
Folder, Date, Reminder, Repeat, Assignee, Labels — each shown only when set, plus one
"Add property" menu that offers the unset ones (and Dependency and Attachment) and reveals
the chosen row already asking its question. Nothing in the panel is dragged or collapsed.
The panel's one "More actions" menu holds Complete task (or Mark as not done), History,
Copy link, Duplicate, Abandon (or Restore) and Delete task…; moving is the Folder row's.
Comments are plain text under their author, and the panel's comment composer attaches no
files: a task's files live in its Attachments section. Pickers open on the answer, not the
instrument — resolved presets first, a calendar or list behind them.

Field rules (limits and their messages: `app/src/shared/tasks.ts`,
`app/src/client/components/task-copy.ts`; a refused value answers 422 with the field
named):

- **Title** is required; an empty commit is refused, not saved.
- **Due date** is a day. The due picker's Time field writes 4.8's reminder on that day,
  never a due_time; a stored v1 due_time is displayed and clearable, never settable.
- **Reminder** and **repeat** require a due date: "Add property" offers them only once the
  task has one.
- **Repeat** is preset-driven (daily, weekdays, weekly on chosen days, every N days or
  weeks, monthly on day 1 to 28, yearly, optional end date); never a raw RRULE box.
- **Folder** offers the real folder library plus "New folder…".
- **Assignee** offers the target folder's members, "Invite by email", and "Unassigned"
  last after a separator; hidden in single-member folders (INV-5).
- **Steps** add, check, rename in place, reorder by drag or keyboard; a step's own "…"
  holds "Make this a task" and "Remove".
- **Attachments** are files and links per 4.16 and 10.3; a pasted URL becomes a link,
  never a fake file.

Clearing the date keeps 8.4's warning verbatim, and moving a task warns when an assignee
would be unassigned (words: `app/src/client/components/task-copy.ts`). "Delete task…" moves the task to Trash
behind a confirmation and then offers the undo bar.

Rows (`app/src/client/components/task-row.tsx`):

- A row carries its section after its folder chip, read as a path, and omits it where the
  row already sits under that section's heading.
- Two densities, one component; two lines is the default because a truncated title does
  not announce itself. The two may differ in height and in where details sit and nothing
  else; a bare task costs one line.
- The details on a row are the controls that change them: the due and repeat chips open
  the card's own pickers.
- One model turns a task's fields into words for row and card alike
  (`app/src/client/tasks/task-model.ts`), and a person's tint is deterministic, so one person is one
  colour everywhere. Wherever a person is drawn, their own picture is drawn; tinted
  initials are the fallback only for a person with none.
- A row's own rename handler, where a surface supplies one, wins over the card's: pressing
  the title renames in place, committing on Enter or blur and abandoning on Escape. A
  press anywhere else on the row opens the task in the panel; a chip opens its own picker.
- An empty block draws the control that fills it rather than nothing.

Entry defaults, derived from the page's own spine so a made task appears where it was made:

| Opened from | defaults |
| - | - |
| The bare strip control | folder Desk, undated |
| Today | folder Desk, a Today entry (membership), dated today |
| Scheduled list | folder Desk, dated today |
| A calendar cell | folder Desk, dated that cell's day |
| Assignments | folder Desk, assigned to the viewer |
| A folder page, its loose block | that folder |
| A section's capture | that folder, that section |
| A board column | that column's value, whatever its axis — folder, person, day, or section |

Boards (`app/src/client/components/task-board.tsx`): a section may carry a mark (a folder's glyph or a
person's face) inline in its heading; Overdue refuses a drop, being a relation to today
rather than a value.
