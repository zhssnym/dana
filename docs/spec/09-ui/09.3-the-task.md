### 9.3 The task (create and edit are one artifact, and the artifact is the task)

There is no task form. A task is its row, its card, and its page: the row expands in place
into the card; the card is the same surface a dialog, a side panel, or a tab presents; and
/app/tasks/:id is that surface as a page. Every edit commits as it is made — no Save
control, no draft state — and 9.1's undo bar covers the destructive ones. The side panel
makes the page narrower rather than covering it. A task opened as a tab carries its own
window-bar controls, with its folder and section at the bar's left.

Where a task opens (dialog, panel, or tab) is an account preference, 4.19's
`user_settings.task_open`, which is the record. The browser keeps a mirror only so the
first task opens in the chosen place before the settings bundle arrives; the bundle wins
and refreshes the mirror, and sign-out clears it — the theme's pattern (9.7;
`app/src/client/lib/task-surface-preference.ts`).

The card is `app/src/client/components/task-card.tsx` and `app/src/client/components/task-surface.tsx`; what it
holds and in what order is there. The rules it is held to: header chips each open their
own picker in place; the footer shows the task's creation and completion from 4.13 (actor,
viewer-local time, "Deleted user" fallback), the rest of its history being the Activity
control; pickers open on the answer, not the instrument — resolved presets first, a
calendar or list behind them.

Field rules (limits and their messages: `app/src/shared/tasks.ts`,
`app/src/client/components/task-copy.ts`; a refused value answers 422 with the field
named):

- **Title** is required; an empty commit is refused, not saved.
- **Due date** is a day. The due picker's Time field writes 4.8's reminder on that day,
  never a due_time; a stored v1 due_time is displayed and clearable, never settable.
- **Reminder** and **repeat** require a due date and are disabled — visible and inoperable,
  with no helper text — until one is set; they stand next to the due chip, so their
  position says what they wait on.
- **Repeat** is preset-driven (daily, weekdays, weekly on chosen days, every N days or
  weeks, monthly on day 1 to 28, yearly, optional end date); never a raw RRULE box.
- **Folder** offers the real folder library plus "New folder…".
- **Assignee** offers the target folder's members, "Invite by email", and "Unassigned"
  last after a separator; hidden in single-member folders (INV-5).
- **Steps** add, check, delete, and reorder by drag or keyboard.
- **Attachments** are files and links per 4.16 and 10.3; a pasted URL becomes a link,
  never a fake file.

Clearing the date keeps 8.4's warning verbatim, and moving a task warns when an assignee
would be unassigned (words: `app/src/client/components/task-copy.ts`). "Delete task…" moves the task to Trash
behind a confirmation and then offers the undo bar.

Rows (`app/src/client/components/task-row.tsx`):

- A row carries its section after its folder chip, read as a path, and omits it where the
  row already sits under that section's heading.
- A row is one line, 32–36px: checkbox, title, then muted meta on the right (date,
  folder only where the list spans folders, assignee face, steps n/m, repeat glyph) and
  the star, drawn on hover and whenever it is set. The meta is text, not controls.
- One model turns a task's fields into words for row and card alike
  (`app/src/client/tasks/task-model.ts`), and a person's tint is deterministic, so one person is one
  colour everywhere. Wherever a person is drawn, their own picture is drawn; tinted
  initials are the fallback only for a person with none.
- Pressing anywhere on a row opens the task; the checkbox and the star are the row's only
  controls. There is no chevron, no row overflow menu and no rename in the row: the task's
  panel carries its title and its one "…" menu. Shift-click or Cmd/Ctrl-click selects
  rows, and a selection shows a bar whose acts are words: Complete, Move, Date, Delete,
  Clear. On a touch screen the star is an indicator only, never a tap target.
- An empty block draws the control that fills it rather than nothing.
- The surface offers the tab only where it can hand the task to the tab strip; Escape
  closes the surface, and the card's footer offers no second close.

Entry defaults, derived from the page's own spine so a made task appears where it was made:

| Opened from | defaults |
| - | - |
| The bare strip control | folder Desk, undated |
| Today | folder Desk, a Today entry (membership), dated today |
| Scheduled list | folder Desk, dated today |
| A calendar cell | folder Desk, dated that cell's day |
| Assignments | folder Desk, assigned to the viewer |
| A folder page, its loose block | that folder |
| A section's capture | that folder, that section |
| A board column | that column's value, whatever its axis — folder, person, day, or section |

Boards (`app/src/client/components/task-board.tsx`): a section may carry a mark (a folder's glyph or a
person's face) inline in its heading; Overdue refuses a drop, being a relation to today
rather than a value.
