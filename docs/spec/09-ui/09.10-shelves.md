### 9.10 Shelves

A shelf is the folder page one level up: a per-user arrangement of folders with a page of
its own — cover, mark (icon and colour), name (inline rename; renaming rewrites the member
labels and re-addresses the page in one act), editable description, and blocks. Folder
rows appear on the page as blocks among the writing: their presence is the membership's
fact, their position the page's arrangement; a folder row under a heading folds with it;
pressing one opens that folder as its own tab. An "Add folder" row offers every folder not
on the shelf (never Desk) and "New folder…". The shelf's overflow carries its contract:
History, Rename, Copy link, Duplicate (the page, no folders — a folder is on one shelf),
Save as workflow, Delete. Deleting a shelf unlabels its folders and keeps them. A shelf
offers no views and no filter: a view redraws tasks and a shelf holds folders; its list is
its label, and narrowing it would be editing it. Shelves are private by construction — no
link to send grants anything, no members exist (DES-195/196); the page's address is still
copyable, an address not being an invitation.

**Annotation, 2026-09-04, issue #118: the shelf's cover is reachable now.** The sentence
above has granted a shelf a cover since RFC 0005 and the surface had no way to choose one:
the picker drew no tab for a shelf, 7.15 being unable to make a picture that was not some
folder's, so "cover" here was a promise the page did not keep and setting one through the
API answered 200 and stored nothing (#100). A shelf now offers the folder page's own three
tiers, on the terms **9.6 states once for all three kinds of page**, and keeps the picture in
4.18's own columns through 7.16's PATCH, which makes it private the way the rest of a shelf
is. AC-406.

**Annotation, 2026-09-04, issue #117: what Duplicate copies, and what it stopped copying.**
The act is one call, `POST /api/v1/shelves/:id/duplicate` (7.16 as amended the same day),
where it was `POST /shelves` followed by a write of the blocks. Three things follow that a
person sees. The copy is named **"<name> (copy)"**, and "(copy 2)" upward where that name is
taken, 4.18's UNIQUE (user_id, name) admitting no second one; the client wrote "<name> copy"
until this date. The copy carries the shelf's **cover**, as a picture of its own rather than
a second reference to the same file, so changing or removing either shelf's cover leaves the
other alone — Imran's ruling on #117, and it could not be done from the client at all,
`POST /shelves` refusing a cover out loud rather than dropping it (#40). And **the copy no
longer carries the shelf's blocks**: the clause above, "Duplicate (the page, no folders — a
folder is on one shelf)", separates the shelf from its membership rather than promising its
prose, which is the reading 7.16's amendment takes and which is recorded here so the change
is met as a decision rather than found as a difference. AC-407 states the act as a person
performs it and AC-387 the endpoint's own halves; both are decisions rather than proofs,
their test files having gone in the 2026-09-20 cut.
