### 9.6 Folder page

The folder page is a page in the full sense: a cover it can be given; its mark (the identity control — pressing it opens the icon-and-colour picker, writing 4.3's shared identity); its name (inline rename, owner only; members read a heading); its editable description; and its body — the loose open tasks with a quick-add, then the page's blocks (sections, paragraphs, images, per 4.17) with tasks assigned to sections, a capture per section, an insert affordance between blocks, and the Done section collapsed at the foot (named "Completed" until the polish round of 2026-08-30: the finished pile was Done in two places and Completed in three, which reads as two states). Its board groups by section, and the column holding the tasks under none is named "No section" where the page has sections and "Tasks" where it has none (amended at the polish round of 2026-08-30: "No section" on a page with no sections read as something missing); while the board is shown the Done section is not drawn beneath it, the Done column being the one place a finished task stands. Ordering within sections and the block arrangement are the folder's, shared by every member; the filter of 9.1 narrows the page without touching its arrangement. The folder's conversation has two homes — a closed section at the page's foot, or the side panel — switched from the surface itself; a message is told apart by tone, never hue; likes are the whole reaction set.

Who may do what, the role matrix (RFC 0005, recording DES-225):

| Act | Owner | Member |
| - | - | - |
| Rename, Delete | yes | no — the name is the folder's address, and delete ends it for everybody |
| Leave | only via ownership transfer (below) | yes |
| Tasks: create, edit, complete, abandon, delete-to-Trash | yes | yes |
| The page: blocks, cover, icon, colour, description | yes | yes — the event log says who |
| History, Copy link, Duplicate, Save as workflow | yes | yes |
| Share, remove a member | per the 5.4 matrix, unchanged | per 5.4 |
| Shelf placement, sidebar arrangement, label colour | personal, always | personal, always |

A surface offers exactly the acts the viewer's role permits — Leave where Delete would be, never both — and the server refuses the rest regardless (10.2). Deleting moves the folder to Trash for every member at once, with the undo bar and no confirmation; members are notified (4.14). Leaving confirms — the way back is an invitation, not a Trash row — writes no Trash entry, offers no undo, and records "Left": "Leave Work? You lose access to it, and anything in it assigned to you becomes unassigned." An owner with other members leaves only by transferring ownership first (7.16), choosing a successor, defaulting to the longest-standing member per 8.9's order; a sole owner is offered Delete instead, 5.3's rule unchanged. Remove-a-member keeps its v1 confirmation verbatim. Each confirmation renders as one sentence, restyled never reworded.

Duplicate takes the structure and never the grants or the progress: tasks, sections, paragraphs, and section assignments copy; members, sharing, the conversation, assignees, and completion do not; spent due dates re-anchor on today and future ones keep, and the bar says which happened (L-055). The copy is the duplicator's own private folder, and its tasks are assigned to whoever made the copy, never left empty, so the copy appears in the duplicator's Assignments (item 44's B10, checked against `domain/acts.ts` at item 51(h) and pinned by AC-277; recorded here at item 57). The History sheet groups by day and names the actor on every line, and its lines and the row's popover are one sentence drawn through one formatter (item 57, D: L-199, L-200).

The sharing panel carries v1's spec unchanged: member list with joined dates and remove controls per 5.4, roster picker, email invite, pending list with Cancel and Resend ("Resent recently" inside 24 hours). It is opened from the Share control in the window bar of 9.1, beside the folder's member faces, rather than from the page's head (polish round, 2026-08-30, folderpage F10); the control is withheld on the default folder, which INV-2 makes single-member, and the faces are drawn only where there is more than one.

The "Label and color" dialog is retired: the folder's icon and colour are the shared identity above, set from the mark; a member's personal shelf placement is "Move to shelf" on the row; and 4.4's per-member color column is no longer written by any v2 surface (existing values are simply unused — the column is frozen and stays).
