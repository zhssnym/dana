### 9.7 Settings

The page (`app/src/client/components/settings-page-content.tsx`) is one column at the page
measure, left-aligned under a plain title, the same frame People and Trash wear
(`components/settings/screen.tsx`). Every row is a label with its control at the far end;
on a phone the control wraps under the label.

Sections, each a named region, in this order:

- **Account** — the Google identity, shown as read from Google; profile picture upload
  (7.15), the picture itself being the control, with "Remove picture" offered exactly while
  there is a picture; and the support address.
- **Appearance** — theme (system, light, dark). The theme is applied to the document before
  first paint from a copy mirrored into the browser, so a reload never flashes; 4.19's
  column stays the record, the mirror is written wherever the bundle is read or written,
  `system` mirrors as `system`, and sign-out clears it (`app/src/client/lib/theme.ts`).
- **Time** — a timezone the viewer chooses, through 7.1's PATCH /me, detected on the first
  session; and the clock (12- or 24-hour).
- **Data** — "Download my data", 7.22's export. The download is an anchor to GET
  /api/v1/me/export carrying the `download` attribute, not a fetch: a navigation carries the
  session cookie and hands 7.22's `Content-Disposition: attachment` to the browser, which
  saves the file under the server's name. The known cost is that the endpoint's rate-limit
  refusal arrives as a downloaded response rather than through 9.1's toast. Then:

  - **Sample data** — one row, drawn once the client knows whether 7.20's sample workspace is
  present, offering Remove or Restore. Remove confirms once with 9.1's dialog, then offers
  the undo bar, whose inverse is POST /sample. This row is the way back for a device that
  dismissed 9.13's strip.
  - **Delete account** — shows its consequences, requires typing DELETE, and says that shared folders the
  viewer owns must first be handed to another member or deleted (Dana names which) and that
  private folders and their tasks are permanently deleted; this matches `DELETE /me`, which
  answers 409 naming every owned shared folder until each is transferred or deleted.

There are no view-pin switches (the sidebar has no pinnable views), and no row density or
"where a task opens" choice: rows are one line and a task opens in the side panel.

People and Trash are reached from the account menu (9.1), not from this page: one screen,
one door.
