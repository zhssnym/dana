### 9.7 Settings

The page is one column at a form's measure rather than at the list measure of 9.1, and
that column is centred in the space the shell gives it. Every row here is a label with its
control at the far end, so the column has to be narrow or the two stop reading as one row;
a narrow column left flush against a wide page then reads as a page that failed to fill.
(Amended at the polish round of 2026-08-30, settings F8, on the owner's note that Settings
"is not centered and leaves too much empty space on the right". The measure itself is
unchanged.)

Sections, each a named region:

- Views — the pin list: every view Dana has, native and saved alike, each with the page's
  own mark, its one-line question, and a pin switch; Desk locked with the note "Desk
  cannot be unpinned: it is where a task goes when you have not said where, so it has to
  stay reachable." Done, Abandoned and Anytime ship unpinned; there is no "Completed"
  entry, Done already being it. - Appearance — the theme control (system, light, dark),
  applied to the document from storage before the application mounts so a reload never
  flashes (L-080, AC-281); row density (comfortable, compact), the control headed "Row
  height" and drawn in the prototype's words as "Two lines" and "One line"; the clock
  (12-hour, 24-hour); where a task opens (dialog, panel, tab). The theme is applied before
  first paint from a copy mirrored into the browser, the 4.19 column staying the record:
  the mirror is written wherever the bundle is read or written, `system` mirrors as
  `system`, and it is cleared at sign-out (RFC 0005 change control items 43 and 52). -
  Account — Google identity display, the name and the address read from it and said to be
  read from it; profile picture upload (7.15), the picture itself being the control, and
  the picture the viewer sets is drawn wherever the viewer is drawn, with a "Remove"
  control beside it offered exactly while there is a picture to take away; a timezone
  control, the viewer's own choice over 7.1's PATCH /me, detected on the first session and
  changeable after; "Download my data", 7.22's export reached as an anchor to GET
  /api/v1/me/export carrying the download attribute, under the line "Everything Dana holds
  about you, as one JSON file."; storage used against the quota of 10.3. (Amended at the polish round
  of 2026-08-30: the timezone was static text, and Sign out stood here as well as in the
  sidebar's account menu, where it now lives alone. Amended again at that round, avatars
  F1: the Remove control, which the screen had drawn absent since the lift because 7.15
  set a picture and cleared none; 7.15 gained DELETE /api/v1/me/avatar under the owner's
  waiver of section 7's freeze and the control is the prototype's own. Amended 2026-09-04,
  issue #162: the export row, which is new work rather than a correction, and 10.9's
  record of the one Google obligation Dana did not meet is why it exists. It is an anchor
  and a navigation rather than a call through the client's fetch layer, because a
  navigation carries the session cookie and hands 7.22's Content-Disposition: attachment
  to the browser, which is what saves the file under the name the server chose; a fetch
  would parse the whole document into memory and then have to turn it back into a file.
  AC-404 asserts the row, the address and the attribute. The endpoint's five-per-hour
  refusal therefore arrives as a downloaded response rather than through 9.1's toast, and
  that is stated here as the known cost of the choice rather than left to be rediscovered:
  nothing in the client sees the status of a navigation.) - People — reached
  from the sidebar alone, its own link there and 9.8's screen behind it (amended at the
  polish round of 2026-08-30: an "Open People" row stood on this page too, making three
  doors to one screen inside a hundred pixels). - Sample data — one row under that
  heading, drawn once the client knows whether the sample workspace of 7.20 is present:
  "Sample workspace present" with Remove sample data, or "Sample workspace removed" with
  Restore sample data. Remove confirms once with 9.1's dialog and then offers the undo
  bar, whose inverse is POST /sample; the row is the way back for a device that dismissed
  the strip of 9.13 (RFC 0005 change control item 60). - Deleting your account — the
  section's heading since RFC 0005 change control item 55, the prototype's own on Imran's
  ruling of 2026-08-22 that "Danger zone" is a coinage naming a feeling rather than its
  contents; Delete account, unchanged from v1 otherwise: two sentences of consequence,
  typed DELETE, then 8.10: "Folders you share will be handed to their longest-standing
  member. Private folders and their tasks are permanently deleted."
