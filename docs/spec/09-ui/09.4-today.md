### 9.4 Today

Structure (`app/src/client/pages/TodayPage.tsx`): the title (no count under it and no View menu), then "Carried over" (what arrived before today
and has not started, and anything overdue, started or not), "Today" (due today or undated,
not started), "In progress" (started and not overdue), "Added to Today" (pulled in, dated
later, not started), and the "New task" row. A heading with no rows is not drawn, and with
only the Today group the day is one list without a heading. A task finished here leaves the
list; finished work is seen on its page's thread (9.6). Empty state per Q1; error
state is the global toast plus inline retry. There is no important-only narrowing.
Today is where a signed-in person lands (9.1), so 9.13's sample strip stands in its top bar and
its starter offer beneath its "New task".

Emphasis rules: an overdue date is in the late colour here as on every screen (9.1),
under "Carried over" too. A row pulled in by a Today entry
offers "Remove from Today", which removes the entry and nothing else. An unfiled task's
folder chip says Desk. Dropping from here confirms that the task leaves Today but stays
everywhere else (`app/src/client/components/ui/copy.ts`).

A task row opens that task, on every page that draws one and whatever the task's status,
through its one page (9.3).
