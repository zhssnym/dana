### 9.4 Today

Structure (`app/src/client/pages/TodayPage.tsx`): the shared page head, then the Carried
group ("Carried over"), the Today group ("Today"), and the recap collapsed at the bottom
("Done today (N)"). Empty state per Q1; error state is the global toast plus inline retry.
Today is where a signed-in person lands (9.1), so 9.13's sample strip stands above it and
its starter offer beneath its list.

The important-only narrowing is the window bar filter's important switch; the page head
carries no control of its own for it, because two controls for one variable forty pixels
apart is how the two disagree. `?state=important` seeds that filter.

Emphasis rules: a carried row keeps its "Carried over" words and drops the red, because a
warning true of every row in the group warns nobody. A row pulled in by a Today entry
offers "Remove from Today", which removes the entry and nothing else. An unfiled task's
folder chip says Desk. Abandoning from here confirms that the task leaves Today but stays
everywhere else (`app/src/client/components/ui/copy.ts`).

A task row opens that task, on every page that draws one and whatever the task's status,
through the surfaces of 9.3.
