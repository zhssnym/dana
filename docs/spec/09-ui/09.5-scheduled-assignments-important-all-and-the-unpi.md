### 9.5 Scheduled, Assignments, Important, All, and the unpinned views

- Scheduled: everything with a date. The list is Q3's order under a ladder of headings —
  Today, Tomorrow, the days of this week, then week and month rungs — headings being
  structure, not controls; a row says "Due tomorrow" about one task and a heading says
  "Tomorrow" about a bucket, one date vocabulary at two altitudes. The calendar keeps Q3C
  whole: day, week, month zooms; previous/next/today; six-week month grid, Monday start;
  all-day before timed; times prefix timed chips at every zoom; ghost styling and the
  banner "Upcoming occurrence of a repeating task"; empty cell creates with that date; "+N
  more" is a control opening the day, which is a look and not a choice: it draws that day
  at the day zoom for as long as the person is on the page and never writes the stored
  zoom, so the next visit opens where they last chose (Imran, 2026-09-04, on the review of
  issue #140). An abandoned chip at month zoom quiets to its glyph
  and a faded tone, the strike appearing only at day and week. Mode persists client-side; zoom
  persists on the account, as 4.10's `calendar_zoom` written through the v2 settings bundle
  (issues #140, #142), with a browser mirror for the first frame only; `?mode=` reaches the
  mode by URL. - Assignments: who is carrying what — every
  open task the viewer can read, grouped by person, the viewer's own group first,
  "Unassigned" last; the "from" half of a row names the creator, Dana recording no
  assigner. The board groups by person; dropping a card on a person assigns them. Capture
  assigns the viewer. The page's spine is a whose-assignments scope control in the window
  bar, resting on everyone: the sidebar entry's name never changes and the value it shows
  lives in that control, the rule every page follows (item 57, D: L-046, L-098). -
  Important: single list per Q4; un-star removes immediately; the star's two names are
  9.1's. On this one page the star rests unfilled, because a filled star on every row of
  the starred page distinguishes nothing, and its tooltip turns outward to name the task's
  home with the fact that home would show, its date for a dated row and its folder for an
  undated one, in 6 Q4's own words. Everywhere else the tooltip confirms importance. -
  All: locks nothing — Q5's union rendered whole, with completed work carried in a
  collapsed section at the foot. Section labels are static. - Done: unpinned by default
  (the pin list governs); day-grouped infinite scroll per Q6; rows drop the spent due-date
  echo under headings that already group by completion; "Mark as not done" with the
  G-SERIES successor confirmation verbatim; no capture anywhere on the page. - Abandoned:
  an unpinned view of the one status with no other home; faded, struck, chip and "Restore"
  per the row's rules; no capture, because a task made pending would be filtered off the
  page it was made on. - Anytime: an unpinned view of undated open work (Q2's rows,
  ungrouped).

Completing is immediate on the wire, as v1 ruled: no write is held back. What v2 adds is
the undo bar of 9.1, which takes a completion back by issuing the inverse transition as a
new mutation — the same "Mark as not done" a person could press on Done, offered for five
seconds where they stand.

**Annotation, 2026-09-04, issues #140 and #142.** "Mode and zoom persist
client-side" is what the Scheduled bullet above read until this date. It is
amended rather than annotated now, the client half having landed; what follows
is the record of why it moved and of what the sentence used to hide.

Zoom now has a durable record on the account: `calendar_zoom` on 4.10, added
by migration 0013, carried by the v2 settings bundle beside the theme, the
density and the clock, with 9.5's own three values and `month` as what a fresh
account reads. Imran ruled it on #140 — the choice should follow the account
across devices rather than reset on the next visit — and ruled out the
browser-only answer because it would create another split record.

**The client half landed the same day and the sentence moved with it** (issue
#140). `PlannedCalendar.tsx` reads the bundle's `calendar_zoom` and writes it
through PATCH on the settings bundle; `src/client/lib/calendar-zoom.ts` keeps a
`localStorage` mirror on the theme's pattern, which is a cache for the first
frame and never a second record, and it is cleared at sign-out beside the theme
and the task surface. This is deliberately the pattern 4.19's `task_open`
amendment records rather than a different one: the record lands first, the
surface follows, and the sentence describing the old behaviour is the sentence
to amend on the day the surface changes. Mode keeps no account record at all and
stays client-side with `?mode=`; only zoom moved.

**The reveal is deliberately outside all of this**, and the distinction is the
ruling rather than a detail of the build. Pressing "+N more" changes what is
drawn; it does not change what the person's calendar is. A control that
rewrote a durable preference as a side effect of looking would carry that
change to every device they own, which is the opposite of what making the zoom
durable was for. So the reveal is transient state, dropped when a zoom is
chosen explicitly or when the calendar leaves the screen, and AC-405 asserts
that a reveal followed by a reload lands on the chosen zoom rather than on day.

**What "persist client-side" was hiding is worth keeping, because it is the
whole finding.** The sentence had been true of mode and false of zoom for as
long as it had existed, and nothing enforced either half, so it drifted in
silence and was found only while fixing something else (#138). AC-405 now
asserts the half that moved, from the browser and across two contexts on one
account, which is what "follows the person rather than the browser" means from
the outside; AC-378 and AC-379 hold the account half over the wire. The half
that did not move, mode, still rests on nobody changing it.
