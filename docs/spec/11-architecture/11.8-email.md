### 11.8 Email

Resend, one provider for the four templates (invite_folder, invite_connection, share_grant, reminder), behind a notification module that logs instead of sending in development and tests; the 11.10 construction seam's options.notifications sink, when injected, replaces that logging so the acceptance suite can observe sends. The 10.7 deliverability checklist applies unchanged. The free tier (3,000 emails per month, 100 per day, as of 2026-07) covers v1; the daily cap is the number to watch if reminder volume grows.

**The list read three until 2026-08-25**, invite serving both the folder invitation of 7.5 and the bare connection invitation, and it splits on Imran's DES-034 ruling (RFC 0005 change control item 32, enacted at S19 under item 34). 7.5 makes folder_id optional, so one name carried two events whose subject, heading, context and icon all differ, and the template name is what the 11.10 sink reads: under one name no acceptance criterion could tell a folder invitation from a connection invitation, and under two it can. The split is decided at the call site from the invite row already in hand rather than by a second read.
