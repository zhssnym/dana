### 1.2 What Dana does as of now

- Google OAuth sign-in, session management, account deletion.
- Folders: one Desk per account plus custom folders; rename (owner); delete to Trash
  (owner); leave (member); icon, colour, cover and description shared by the folder; page
  blocks; per-member shelf placement; completion count; duplicate; save and import as a
  workflow file.
- Sharing: email invites (with or without a folder attached), roster connections, instant
  sharing to connected people, invite cancel, resend, decline; sharing triggered by
  assigning to a non-member.
- Tasks: title, notes, important, due date (day-level in the interface; a stored due time
  is honored), recurrence (optionally end-bounded), one reminder at a time of day, steps,
  assignee (assignable and unassignable), comments with likes and files; three statuses
  (pending, completed, abandoned) with restore; delete to Trash; duplicate; a URL of its
  own.
- Pages: Today, Scheduled (list, board and calendar) and Assignments, governed by the pin
  list; folder pages, Desk's among them; shelf pages; Trash; global search; saved views.
- Activity and notifications: every mutation records an event, a folder and any object can
  answer what happened to it, and the bell carries the viewer's own notifications with a
  read state (sections 4.13, 4.14, 7.12, 9.12).
- Files: images and documents attached to comments and page blocks, served only to people
  who can already read what they hang on, under a per-account quota (sections 4.16, 7.15,
  10.3).
- Email notifications: invite, share grant, reminder. Nothing else.
- PWA: installable shell, offline banner, online-only data.
