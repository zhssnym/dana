### 12.21 Transactional email

The four templates, their two parts, the facts each names, and where its one link lands.

- AC-251 Given any of the four transactional emails, then it renders an HTML part and a text/plain part; each part carries exactly one action link and it is the same link in both; the text part says what the HTML part says rather than being a stub; and the provider send carries both parts (10.7 as amended by RFC 0005 change control item 26).
- AC-252 Given a folder invitation and a bare connection invitation, then the two are sent under different template names, so the 11.10 sink can tell one from the other; each names the person who sent it, the folder invitation naming the folder it invites to and the connection invitation naming no folder; a resend of either is the same template as its create and names the same facts; and where a `display_name` cannot be resolved, "Someone" stands in its place rather than nothing.
- AC-253 Given a folder shared with somebody, then the share-grant email names the person who shared it and the folder they shared, in its subject and in both parts, identically at the 7.4 member add and at the 7.5 instant-share conversion, which are one template by item 32's ruling.
- AC-254 Given either invitation, then it carries item 32's orientation footer verbatim and closes with nothing else; given the share grant or the reminder, then neither carries any of it; and on no template is that line a link, an unsubscribe or a "learn more" being the second action link 10.7 forbids.
- AC-255 Given a reminder for a task with a stored due date and wall time, then the email renders that stored date and that wall time, never "Today" or any other relative wording, and names the folder the task is in; given a date-only task, then it renders the date and no time, the reminder's own `time_of_day` not being a due time (4.4, 8.1, 8.6).
- AC-256 Given each of the four templates, then its one action link lands where item 32 sends it: both invitations on 10.6's start URL, where 9.8's gate meets the recipient because that gate deliberately has no path of its own; the share grant on `/app/folders/:id` for the folder it granted, under a button naming that folder; and the reminder on `/app/tasks/:id` for the task that fired.
