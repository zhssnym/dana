**What was wrong, and it is the reason six criteria are worth their space.**
`reclaimUnwornPictures` in `app/src/server/domain/files.ts` permanently deletes a
folder-less picture and its bytes thirty days after the last thing that named it stopped
naming it. It is the only code path in Dana that destroys a person's data on a clock rather
than on a request, and on 2026-09-20 nothing in the suite ran it at all: AC-385, AC-386 and
AC-413's last clause say what it does and none of the three has an executable form. What
stands between a picture somebody is looking at and that delete is one hand-written

each, age it to **thirty-one days and spare it**, and take an identically aged twin that
nothing holds — so the reference is the only difference between the two — and each pins the
window at **twenty-nine days kept and thirty-one taken**. Every one of them asserts the
`files` row and the object in the store rather than the count the pass answers, because a
sweep that reports four deletions while deleting nothing satisfies any assertion over its
return value.

**AC-705 is the other question, and it was added on 2026-09-20 after an adversarial
review of the five.** Those five are the whole of *which references count* and none of
*what is sweepable at all*, and two clauses decide the second: `kind IN ('cover', 'image')`

reads as a faithful restatement of `files_folderless_kind_check`.

`app/tests/guards/guard-reclaim-reference-arms.test.ts` is the seventh thing and not a
