### 12.7 Today

Q1's membership, the pull, and the zone Today is computed in. The rest of the views are
12.8.

- AC-044 Given POST my-day twice then DELETE my-day twice, then one entry existed with its original added_on, and every call returned 200 (INV-12).
- AC-045 Given a task due today assigned to the viewer, when GET /views/my-day, then it is in the Today group.
- AC-046 Given a pending task due three days ago assigned to the viewer, then it is in the Carried group on every subsequent day.
- AC-047 Given a task assigned to someone else pulled by the viewer, then it appears in the viewer's My Day with an assignee chip, and in nobody else's.
- AC-048 Given an unassigned task due today in a multi-member folder, then it is in no one's My Day and carries the unassigned chip in Planned.
- AC-049 Given a task assigned to the viewer due next week completed today, then it appears in the recap section today.
- AC-050 Given the instant 2026-07-23 20:30 UTC and a task due 2026-07-23 assigned to the viewer, then with tz Asia/Karachi it is Carried (local 2026-07-24) and with tz Europe/London it is Today (local 2026-07-23).

**AC-046 and AC-050 are asserted by their content rather than at the instant they name.**
AC-046's "on every subsequent day" and AC-050's named instant both require owning the clock,
and the acceptance suite deliberately does not: faking it would decide, on behalf of an
implementer who has not yet written a line, that today(V) is read from the process clock
rather than from the database, which 8.1's "the server computes it" admits either way, so the
test would fail against half of the correct implementations. AC-046 is asserted as the
property its clause follows from, that Q1's predicate carries no upper bound on how far past
a due_date may be and that 8.2's qualify_date minimum keeps a pulled overdue row in Carried.
AC-050 keeps the criterion's structure, one task and one instant read under two zones landing
in two groups, with a zone pair whose offsets differ by exactly 24 hours and which observes no
DST, so the relationship holds at every instant instead of in the few hours a day when Karachi
and London happen to differ; the named pair is asserted too, in both directions available
without the clock. Neither criterion is reinterpreted. The clock is the only thing given up,
and it is given up because owning it would test the implementer's choice rather than this
specification's rule.

- AC-132 Given POST /tasks/:id/my-day with tz, then added_on is today in that zone rather than in the account's stored zone, and the pulled task appears in the Today group of a My Day read in the same zone; given an absent or invalid tz, then 422 naming the field and no entry is written.
- AC-165 Given a Today row whose inclusion came from an entry, then it carries "Remove from Today"; given one that also qualifies automatically, then it does not, and using the control removes the row at once.
- AC-355 Given a task not on Today, when its row's More actions offers "Add to Today" and it is pressed, then `POST /tasks/:id/my-day` is written and the menu then offers "Remove from Today" alone (spec 7.8, 9.4).
- AC-450 Given an assigned open task whose Schedule carries a Start Date, then Today's automatic clause admits it from that date onward and not before: on the day before its start it is in neither group, on its start date it is in Today, and on later days it is in Carried while it stays open; a Start-only task behaves the same way and a task carrying neither date never qualifies from age; a due-only task keeps the group it had; reading the page changes no status, no star and no assignment, and the card still carries a future due_date beside the spent start, so nothing derives overdue from a start that has passed; and the manual pull remains its own mechanism, a task starting tomorrow reaching the page through an entry alone; and the Carried group is ordered by Q1's own key, the due date ascending nulls last, so a row that started most recently and is due first leads a row that started first and is due later (spec 6 Q1, 8.1, 8.2; issue #217).

**AC-450 asserts the group and not only the membership, and that is the criterion's
point.** The build issue #217 names and refuses is a second qualification path: leave
`due_date <= today` where it stands and OR a start-date clause beside it. Every "it
appears on its start date" assertion passes against that build, and 8.2's qualify_date
then has two candidate dates for one row, so the Carried/Today split stops being one
rule. The criterion is written so that only the moved clause passes it, and each group is
compared as a whole set so that a widened predicate flooding the page fails whether or
not anybody thought of the row that flooded it.

**The ordering clause was added on 2026-09-20 and the criterion could not see it before.**
Q1's leading key is a decision this slice made explicitly - the due date, not the
activation date Q3 moved to - and the two rows the file first ordered over activate on the
same day, so both keys and `created_at` behind them produce the same sequence. The
criterion passed under either rule and pinned neither. A third carried row whose
activation and due dates rank in opposite directions is what makes the decision visible,
and reverting Q1's ORDER BY to the activation date now fails this criterion rather than
nothing at all.
