### 12.20 Deletion, Trash and restore

The soft delete, the Trash page, restore, the permanent delete, and the 30-day pass. Who
may delete a folder is 12.4, as is AC-843, the custom Folder facet's life in Trash.

- AC-014 Given the owner deletes a custom folder, then the response carries 7.4's exact counts, the folder leaves every list and view, and it waits in Trash with its memberships, tasks of all statuses, steps, My Day entries, reminders, and pending invites held; when the 30 day window lapses and the pass runs, then the folder, all of those rows, and the folder's invites are gone, bare invites and other folders untouched.
- AC-042 Given a task deletion, then the task leaves every list and view and waits in Trash with its steps, My Day entries, and reminder held for restore, any invite keeping its task_id; when the 30 day window lapses and the pass runs, then the row and those dependents are gone and the invite survives with task_id null.
- AC-189 Given a task, when it is deleted, then deleted_at is set, the row leaves every section 6 query, and it appears in the Trash listing of every member of its folder.
- AC-190 Given a trashed task, when a different member of its folder restores it, then the task returns exactly once with its fields intact.
- AC-191 Given a trashed folder 30 days old, when the 11.7 pass runs, then the folder and its tasks are permanently gone; given one 29 days old, then both remain.
- AC-192 Given a folder owner, when they delete the folder, then deleted_at is set and each other member gains a notification; given a non-owner member attempting the delete, then 403 and no change.
- AC-224 Given Trash holding a deleted folder, when Restore is pressed, then the folder returns to the sidebar exactly once with its tasks.
- AC-262 Given a deleted task and a deleted folder in the Trash, then each row names the days it has left of 4.3 and 4.5's thirty, says its kind as a glyph rather than as a word, and still says where it lived (spec 9.11).
- AC-274 Given a trashed task with steps, a conversation and a file hung on that conversation, when the folder owner deletes it permanently, then the task, its steps, its comments and the file's row and bytes are all gone, the Trash no longer lists it, and no restore can bring it back; and given a task that is not in Trash, then the same call is 404, the live delete being 7.6's soft one.
- AC-275 Given a trashed shared folder holding tasks, a page, a stored file and a second member, when its owner deletes it permanently, then the folder, its tasks, its page blocks, its memberships and its files rows are gone, the file's bytes are absent from the store, neither member's Trash lists it, and no restore can bring it back.
- AC-276 Given a trashed task and a trashed folder in a shared folder, when a member who is not the owner asks for either to be deleted permanently, then the answer is 403 and both rows still wait in Trash; and given a person who is not a member at all, then the answer is 404, ownership being invisible to those without it.
- AC-283 Given a shared folder the viewer owns, when "Delete page" is pressed, then one dialog asks whether to hand it to another member or delete it for everyone; when "Hand it to a member…" is chosen, then the client writes nothing yet and opens the transfer picker, which offers exactly the members who currently hold Full control and says so when there are none; when "Delete for everyone" is chosen, then the client writes `DELETE /folders/:id`, no membership is touched, and the folder waits in Trash; given a folder the owner is alone in, then no question is asked and the soft delete is written at once; and given a member who holds Full control without owning the folder, then no question is asked of them either, custody being nothing they can pass on.
- AC-284 Given a shared folder the viewer is a member of and does not own, then the folder's menu offers "Leave page…" and no "Delete page", and pressing it tells them they lose access rather than that the folder is destroyed; and given a task of that folder in Trash, then the viewer's Trash row offers Restore and no permanent delete, the act being the owner's.
- AC-285 Given a trashed task in a folder the viewer owns, when the row's permanent delete is pressed, then a confirmation names the task and says the act cannot be undone, and its "Delete forever" writes `DELETE /api/v1/trash/tasks/:id` with the CSRF header, after which the row is gone from Trash and the task answers 404; and given the same for a trashed folder, then the address is `DELETE /api/v1/trash/folders/:id`; and given "Empty trash", then its confirmation counts the rows it would remove and "Cancel" removes none.
- AC-337 Given Trash holding a deleted task, then its tab is named "Trash", the row's Restore is drawn at rest rather than on hover, and pressing it raises a toast naming the folder the task went back to (spec 9.11, 9.1).
- AC-351 Given two tasks selected in a folder, when the selection bar's Delete is pressed, then the confirmation reads "Move 2 tasks to Trash? They stay there for 30 days." over controls named "Delete" and "Keep them"; and when it is confirmed, then one undo entry reads "Deleted 2 tasks" and its Undo restores both (spec 7.6, 7.13, 9.1, 9.3).
- AC-357 Given an open task, then its footer's More actions names History, Abandon and "Delete task…" as the row's does; and when Delete is confirmed over "Move this task to Trash? It stays there for 30 days.", then one `DELETE /tasks/:id` is written, the surface closes, and the row's undo entry stands (spec 9.3, 7.6, 7.13).
