### 8.1 Timezone anchors and today

Model: floating dates and times everywhere a human plans; UTC instants everywhere the
system records an event.

- Every request that evaluates, stores or filters by a date carries tz, the client's
  current IANA zone. today(V) = the current date in tz at the moment of evaluation. The
  server computes it; the client never filters by date itself. An endpoint that works with
  a date and takes no tz is an incomplete surface, never a licence to substitute the stored
  zone below.
- users.timezone exists for one consumer only: reminder fire-instant computation for that
  user as assignee. It auto-syncs at every successful login that carries the 7.1 tz
  parameter, and whenever a session reports a different zone through PATCH /me; a login
  carrying no tz syncs nothing, and a reported zone that is a tzdb alias of the stored one
  is not a different zone: no write, and no 8.6 recompute. There is no manual lock in v1.
  Traveling therefore moves both today and future reminder instants to the new zone.
- The day boundary is local midnight in tz. DST changes move that instant but never break
  date arithmetic, because comparisons are date against date, never instant against
  instant.
- Boundary statement, exact: a task with due_date D is due today when D = today(V),
  overdue when D < today(V), future when D > today(V). Overdue, due today and future are
  relations to the **deadline** only, so a start_date passing never makes a task overdue
  and a Start-only task has no deadline to pass.
- Today's automatic clause admits A <= today(V), where A is the task's **activation
  date**: its start_date where it has one and its due_date otherwise. Today begins showing
  a task the day its work becomes active. tasks_start_before_due_check makes A <= D
  wherever both exist.

**Assumption: silent timezone auto-sync is acceptable.** Travel moves today and moves
reminder instants, without asking. If travellers find that wrong, the fix is a per-user
timezone lock: one column and one guard in the sync.
