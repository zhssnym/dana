### 6.1 Partition proof

Claim: Q2 and Q3 partition Q5 with no gap and no overlap. Proof: all three share the
identical scope predicate, folder_id IN ACCESSIBLE(V) AND status IN (pending, abandoned).
Q3 adds SCHEDULED = (start_date IS NOT NULL OR due_date IS NOT NULL); Q2 adds NOT
SCHEDULED. `IS NOT NULL` yields a real boolean for every row and never UNKNOWN, so the
disjunction is a real boolean too and its negation is total: for any row in Q5's result
exactly one of SCHEDULED and NOT SCHEDULED holds, exclusively and exhaustively, so the
row appears in exactly one of Q2 and Q3. Conversely any row in Q2 or Q3 satisfies Q5's
predicate by construction. Hence Q5 = Q2 union Q3 and Q2 intersect Q3 is empty. The proof
depends on the three scopes staying textually identical and on Q2's conjunct remaining
the negation of Q3's.

**Only the second of those two dependencies has a test, and the correction is worth more
than the sentence it replaces.** This read "acceptance tests AC-070 and AC-451 pin those"
when issue #216 landed. AC-451 is real and pins the second dependency over all four
Schedule states, both directions of the partition, and the row count that separates a
union from a partition. **AC-070 has no test file**: it was one of the 221 criteria cut
on 2026-09-20 under the ruling recorded in `app/tests/README.md`, so the scope-identity
dependency — the part of this proof that a copied-and-edited scope predicate would break
— is pinned by nothing at all. What stands in for it is textual and structural rather
than behavioural: the three queries build on one exported `OPEN_SCOPE` fragment in
`app/src/server/views/cards.ts` and add their own conjunct to it, so a scope that drifts
has to be written as a deliberate second copy. That is weaker than a test and is said
here plainly rather than left as a citation to something that does not exist.

**The second dependency is issue #216's, and it is why the conjunct is written as a
negation.** Until 2026-09-20 the two conjuncts were `due_date IS NULL` and `due_date IS
NOT NULL`, a partition of one column's nullness that any reader can check by eye.
Scheduled is now a disjunction over two nullable columns, and its complement written out
in full - `start_date IS NULL AND due_date IS NULL` - is a second sentence that has to be
kept in step with the first by hand. Written as `NOT SCHEDULED` over the one fragment,
the two cannot disagree, and the proof above rests on a law rather than on an inspection.
`app/src/server/views/cards.ts` is where that one fragment lives.

Coverage corollary: every task row is visible somewhere. Open rows (pending, abandoned)
appear in Q5 and their home (Q2 or Q3); completed rows appear in Q6. No status escapes
every page.
