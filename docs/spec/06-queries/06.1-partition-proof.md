### 6.1 Partition proof

Claim: Q2 and Q3 partition Q5 with no gap and no overlap. Proof: all three share the
identical scope predicate, folder_id IN ACCESSIBLE(V) AND status IN (pending, abandoned).
Q2 adds due_date IS NULL; Q3 adds due_date IS NOT NULL. For any row in Q5's result,
due_date is a single column that is either null or not null, exclusively and exhaustively;
therefore the row satisfies exactly one of the two added conjuncts, so it appears in
exactly one of Q2 and Q3. Conversely any row in Q2 or Q3 satisfies Q5's predicate by
construction. Hence Q5 = Q2 union Q3 and Q2 intersect Q3 is empty. The proof depends only
on the three scopes staying textually identical; acceptance test AC-070 pins that with a
property check.

Coverage corollary: every task row is visible somewhere. Open rows (pending, abandoned)
appear in Q5 and their home (Q2 or Q3); completed rows appear in Q6. No status escapes
every page.
