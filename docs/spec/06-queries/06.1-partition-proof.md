### 6.1 Partition proof

Claim: Q2 and Q3 partition Q5 with no gap and no overlap. Proof: all three share the
identical scope predicate, folder_id IN ACCESSIBLE(V) AND status IN (pending, abandoned).
Q3 adds SCHEDULED = (start_date IS NOT NULL OR due_date IS NOT NULL); Q2 adds NOT
SCHEDULED. `IS NOT NULL` yields a real boolean for every row and never UNKNOWN, so the
disjunction is a real boolean too and its negation is total: for any row in Q5's result
exactly one of SCHEDULED and NOT SCHEDULED holds, exclusively and exhaustively, so the
row appears in exactly one of Q2 and Q3. Conversely any row in Q2 or Q3 satisfies Q5's
predicate by construction. Hence Q5 = Q2 union Q3 and Q2 intersect Q3 is empty. The proof
depends on the three scopes staying textually identical and on Q2's conjunct remaining
the negation of Q3's.

Both dependencies are held structurally: the queries build on one scope fragment and Q2's
conjunct is written as `NOT SCHEDULED` over Q3's, so the two cannot disagree. Fragments:
`OPEN_SCOPE`, `SCHEDULED` and `ANYTIME` in `app/src/server/views/cards.ts`.

Coverage corollary: every task row is visible somewhere. Open rows (pending, abandoned)
appear in Q5 and their home (Q2 or Q3); completed rows appear in Q6. No status escapes
every page.
