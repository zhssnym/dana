### 10.2 Authorization enforcement points

**Corrected 2026-08-31 against the code. The previous sentence opened "One chokepoint:"
and there is not one.** There cannot be, because what counts as authorized depends on what
is being asked for, so no single middleware can decide it. What the sentence described as
one mechanism is two, and the difference matters to anyone adding a route.

**The structural half.** Every data query joins folder_members on the viewer *inside the
FROM clause*, so a query that forgets the join returns nothing rather than returning
another person's rows. This half is genuinely hard to get wrong, and it is why the
original sentence felt true.

**The hand-written half.** Every mutation resolves the 5.4 matrix server-side before
touching rows, through roughly twenty `accessOf(...)` gates written by hand across nine
files. **Adding a route means adding its gate. Nothing adds it for you, and nothing fails
if you forget.** `app/tests/ac-236-structural-guard-sweep.test.ts` walks the mounted
router and refuses any POST, PATCH, PUT or DELETE that lacks a session or CSRF check.
**It covers GET as well, corrected 2026-09-02 under issue #33**, where every read must
answer 401 without a session unless it is one of three named public routes, each carrying
in the test the reason it can have no session: the platform health probe and the two legs
of the OAuth sign-in. That allowlist is asserted in both directions, so an entry naming a
route that is in fact gated, or a route that no longer exists, fails rather than widening
quietly. **What the sweep checks is still authentication, not authorization**, and no test
anywhere asserts that a given route gates on membership: a GET that requires a session and
then reads any row it is asked for passes everything here.
Inaccessible resources answer 404 (never 403) to avoid existence leaks;
permitted-visibility but forbidden-action answers 403. No endpoint trusts a folder_id,
task_id, or user_id from the client without these checks. The composite FK of 4.5 and the
constraints of section 4 are the last line, not the first.
