### 9.8 People and invites

People, a section of Settings (`app/src/client/components/settings/people.tsx`, 9.7),
at /app/settings#people; the old /app/people redirects there:
the roster with a remove control behind a confirmation saying shared pages stay visible
and a fresh invite is needed to share new ones; and a bare invite by email, one "Invite by email" row whose Invite button opens the
field in its place (Esc folds it back), refused when the address is already a connection
(7.5's 409). Pending bare invites and connections are rows of the same shape. Nothing in the roster says human; an
entry is an entry whatever it is. The People section carries no incoming invite list (see below).

Assignment chip: rows and the card show "Invite pending: {email}" while an
assignment-invite is outstanding.

**Pending invites** (`app/src/client/pages/InviteGate.tsx`). While Q12 is non-empty, the
pending invites show as rows at the top of the page column on every /app path, above the
page; the rest of the app stays usable. Each row reads "Hassan invited you to Wedding" (or
"Hassan invited you to connect") with Decline and Accept, and is addressed from that
sentence, which tells a reader and a test which Accept belongs to which invite. Answering
the last invite removes the rows; accepting a page invite lands inside that page.

No invite list elsewhere, and in particular none on the People screen: the rows already show
at every /app path, /app/settings included, so a second list would give a person two places
to answer one invite.
