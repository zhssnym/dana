# Founders' Agreement

Project: Dana  
Parties: Hassan and Imran  
Start date: 2026-07-29  
Revised 2026-09-23: shortened, workflow rules removed, with both partners' agreement.

## 1. What This Is

- A working agreement between two people building a product together, not a company or a formal legal contract.
- Written down so nothing important rests on assumptions, not out of distrust.
- Both partners are owners of Dana and build it together.

## 2. Goals

- **Imran:** found a productivity platform people rely on; learn to take a product from idea to real users; grow Dana into something stronger than a chatbot or a to-do app, and earn a place in the future of AI and technology.
- **Hassan:** solve real problems with meaningful technical work and learn how startups work. Scale and revenue matter less; ten people relying on Dana daily would be a huge success.

## 3. Roles and Decision Rights

Roles are not rigid: either partner can help, suggest, raise concerns and share tasks by mutual understanding.

**Imran**
- Product direction: what Dana becomes, which features are added or removed, and in what order. May request features or changes at any time.
- Finances: full autonomy over what to fund, renew or cancel. Money spent counts as Imran's contribution; no shared ledger beyond agreed recurring payments.
- UI/UX: design, branding and behaviour across mobile, web and desktop, including a clear non-technical description of each requested feature and, where appropriate, a prototype.

**Hassan**
- Technical design: architecture, APIs, data, integrations, security, constraints and edge cases.
- Implementation and reliability: code, testing, deployment and maintenance.
- Technical documentation and consistent engineering practice.
- Final say on technical decisions, aiming for practical approaches and flexible foundations.
- Explains effort, time and cost of significant requests so Imran can decide; then builds it as well as possible.

## 4. Working Together

- Talk before both partners work on the same thing.
- Gaps found during development are discussed and decided together.

## 5. Feature Requests

- Explained well enough before work starts; "I want an xyz feature" alone is not enough.
- Written in plain language a non-technical person understands. No vague or subjective wording such as "it should feel good" or "look modern".
- Shown through a prototype where possible.
- Small features need little documentation: a short explanation, issue or conversation can be enough.
- Hassan explains the cost; Imran decides whether it is worth it.

## 6. Time Commitment

- No minimum hours. A serious side project alongside studies, work, family and life.
- Commitment means completing the agreed scope, on each partner's own schedule.
- Reply within about 72 hours, even if only "I can't get to this this week".
- Either partner can declare a blackout period (exams, work, personal matters). No work is expected then, the other partner respects it without pressure, and responsibilities can be adjusted by agreement.

## 7. Ownership and Partnership

- Equal partners. Contribution is measured by responsibility and value, not hours, commits or tasks.
- No transferring, selling, giving away or promising any part of Dana's ownership without discussing and agreeing together.
- Formal arrangements (vesting, investors, dilution) are agreed together if Dana is incorporated, raises investment or earns significant revenue.
- Partnership stands unless a partner explicitly leaves. More than one month unresponsive, with no reason or declared unavailability, may be treated as stepping away, but only after a reasonable effort to communicate.

## 8. Ownership of Work

- Everything made for Dana (code, docs, designs, branding, domains, accounts) belongs to Dana as a jointly built project.
- A partner who leaves cannot restrict Dana from using work contributed while involved.
- Dana's proprietary code, private systems or assets are not copied into a competing product without discussing it first.
- Reusing general knowledge, skills, methods and lessons from Dana elsewhere, including competing projects, is fine and expected.
- A LICENSE file may document ownership and usage terms.

## 9. Principles and Disputes

- Work and major decisions stay within Islamic moral principles; Dana will not knowingly be built around anything clearly prohibited or unethical under them.
- A serious disagreement over whether a major decision meets those principles is first resolved together; failing that, guidance from a qualified, mutually trusted authority is the final reference.

## 10. Agreement

Both partners have read, understood and agreed to this document. Perfection is not expected; commitment is to communicate, contribute, learn, solve problems together, respect each other's work, and give Dana a serious chance to become useful.

We hereby confirm and acknowledge this document as duly signed.
